# 03 — Movement Map

## Song: ZOOMY ZOOM FREEZE!
## BPM: 110 | Time Signature: 4/4

---

## Movement Set (6 core actions)

| # | Action Name | Description | Body Parts | Cue Word |
|---|-------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Zoom** | Run in place fast, pump arms forward/back | Legs, arms | "Zoomy zoom zoom" |
| 2 | **Freeze** | Complete full-body stop, hold pose like a statue | Whole body | "FREEZE!" |
| 3 | **Slow-mo** | Exaggerated slow walk, wide slow steps | Legs, arms | "Slow", "snail", "turtle" |
| 4 | **Wiggle** | Shake entire body side to side, finger flutter | Whole body, hands, toes | "Wiggle", "shake" |
| 5 | **Spin** | Turn around once in place | Whole body | "Spin" |
| 6 | **Jump** | Jump up with both feet, land soft | Legs | "Jump", "HIGH" |

## Accessibility Notes for Ages 2–5
- Every action is do-able standing still in a small space
- No coordination required between hands and feet simultaneously
- Freeze is explicitly cued with word + visual character stop
- Count-based bridge (1-2-3) gives entry point for non-verbal participation
