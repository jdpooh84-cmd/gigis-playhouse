## Scene Notes

### S01 — Intro
- Characters bounce into frame from opposite sides
- Ziggy waves at camera ("at viewer")
- Bop does a little boing jump
- Both strike a "ready!" pose on last beat before Hook 1

### S02 — Hook 1
- CRITICAL: characters must SNAP to freeze on every "FREEZE!" word
- Exaggerated frozen poses (Bop makes funny surprise face)
- Movement is big and clear so children can copy

### S03–S04 — Verse 1
- Ziggy leads the action cues
- Bop follows slightly behind (mimicking behavior modeled for children)
- Keep movement broad, in center of frame

### S06 — Verse 2 Slow-mo
- This is the comedic scene — exaggerated slow walk looks funny
- Ziggy lifts each foot in slow motion with exaggerated expression
- Bop's mouth is wide open in exaggerated slow-mo

### S09 — Bridge
- Camera (frame) stays still
- Characters perform 1-2-3-FREEZE twice
- Each number has a clear distinct action
- FREEZE hold is longest pause (1.5 beats)

### S11 — Outro
- Return to Meadow (bookend with Intro)
- Characters wave directly at camera
- Final freeze: both hold funny silly poses
- Bop: arms out, one leg up
- Ziggy: paws on cheeks, surprised face
