# 01 — APIFY Research Summary

## Research Method
Two APIFY RAG Web Browser runs searching:
1. "high performing preschool action song YouTube Shorts 2024 2025 patterns hook repetition BPM movement engagement"
2. "children action song video structure 2-4 minutes freeze dance wiggle jump preschool YouTube 2024 engagement patterns viral"

## Key Findings

### Top Performing Pattern: Freeze Dance
- **Pattern**: Music plays → children dance → music stops → children FREEZE
- **Why it works**: Combines movement, listening skill, and self-regulation
- **Confirmed by**: Lah-Lah x Bounce Patrol (Instagram), Kiboomers (YouTube), Danny Go (YouTube)
- **Caption evidence**: "FREEZE DANCE = instant brain break" — Lah-Lah Kids Music account

### Title Patterns
- Descriptive + action word: "Dance & Stop Song", "Wiggle, Bounce and Shake"
- Audience callout: "Most Fun FREEZE DANCE", "Spring Movement & Action Songs for Preschool"
- Verb-led: "Action Songs for Kids"

### Thumbnail Patterns
- Bright characters with exaggerated expressions
- Movement blur or pose
- Bold sans-serif title overlay, high contrast

### Pacing Patterns
- Movement instruction changes every 4–8 beats
- Verbal cue precedes action by 1–2 beats ("NOW... wiggle!")
- Clear transition sounds between sections (often a drum hit or bell)

### Hook/Repetition Patterns
- Single hook phrase repeated 3–5 times per chorus
- Hook must be singable AND do-able simultaneously
- "When the music plays… DANCE! When it stops… FREEZE!" is the dominant format
- Call-and-response structure outperforms pure narration

### Video Length Patterns
- Most successful: 2–3 minutes (toddler attention window)
- Extended: Up to 5 minutes with movement variety
- Target: 2.5 minutes core + optional loop extension

### Movement-Driven Engagement Patterns
- Speed contrast (fast/slow) is highly re-watchable
- Body parts named explicitly build vocabulary
- Count-based sequences (1, 2, 3) give predictable engagement spikes
- "FREEZE" moments are screenshot and share moments

## Locked Decisions From Research

| Parameter | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Topic | Fast/slow/freeze movement |
| Hook concept | "Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!" — speed + stop game |
| Movement family | Speed contrast + freeze |
| Rhyme scheme | AABB with call-and-response refrain |
| Pacing approach | 8-bar verse blocks, hook every 16 bars, count bridge at 2/3 mark |

## What Was NOT Copied
- No existing song titles, lyrics, or hooks used
- No brand names (Kiboomers, Danny Go, Bounce Patrol) referenced in content
- Structural patterns only were extracted, not content
