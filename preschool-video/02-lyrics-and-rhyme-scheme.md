# 02 — Lyrics and Rhyme Scheme

## Song Title
**ZOOMY ZOOM FREEZE!**

## Rhyme Scheme
AABB — rhymed couplets throughout verses
Hook: AAAB pattern (three "FREEZE" end-lines + a couplet refrain)

## BPM
110

## Full Lyric Sheet

---

### INTRO (4 bars, spoken/sung)
```
Hey hey hey! Are you ready to play?
Let's move our bodies today!
Get up, stand up, let's GO!
Zoomy zoom zoom — here we go!
```

---

### HOOK (repeated 4x in song)
```
Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!        [A]
Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!        [A]
Can you do it, pretty please?     [A-rhyme]
Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!        [A]
```

---

### VERSE 1 (8 bars)
```
Run in place, here we GO!                    [A]
Pump those arms up high and low!             [A]
Wiggle your fingers, wiggle your toes!       [B]
Everybody shake from your head to your nose! [B]
```
*Action cues: run in place, arm pumps, finger wiggle, full body shake*

ONE... [jump]

| Lyric phrase | Action |
|---|---|
| "Zoomy zoom zoom" | Run in place, fast arms |
| "FREEZE!" | Full body stop, hold pose |
| "Run in place, here we GO!" | Run in place |
| "Pump those arms up high and low!" | Arm pumps |
| "Wiggle your fingers, wiggle your toes!" | Finger + toe wiggle |
| "Everybody shake from head to nose!" | Full body shake |
| "Now go SLOW like a sleepy snail" | Very slow exaggerated walk |
| "Walk like a turtle along the trail!" | Slow walk continues |
| "Spin around — one, two, three!" | Spin in place |
| "Jump up HIGH and land with me!" | Jump + land |
| "ONE... TWO... THREE... FREEZE!" | Count each action, freeze |
