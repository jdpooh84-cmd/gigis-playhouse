# 00 — Concept Summary

## Project Title
**ZOOMY ZOOM FREEZE!**

## Episode Overview
A 2.5–3 minute original cartoon action song for ages 2–5. Two cartoon animal friends — Ziggy (a round blue cat) and Bop (a round orange frog) — guide toddlers through a fast/slow/freeze movement game set to a punchy 110 BPM beat.

## Target Age
2–5 years

## Topic
Fast/slow/freeze movement — speed contrast and body control

## Core Hook
"Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!"

## Why This Works
- Speed contrast (fast vs. slow) is cognitively engaging and physically accessible at age 2–5
- "Freeze" creates a game element — toddlers love the sudden stop
- Supports self-regulation, listening, and proprioception
- Supported by APIFY research: freeze dance is #1 reusable preschool action-song format

## Rhyme Scheme
AABB (rhymed couplets) with a repeating call-and-response hook

## BPM
110

## Runtime Target
2 minutes 30 seconds to 3 minutes

## Movement Set (6 actions)
1. Zoom — run in place fast, pump arms
2. Freeze — sudden stop, hold still
3. Slow-mo walk — exaggerated slow walk
4. Wiggle — shake whole body
5. Spin — turn around in place
6. Jump — jump up and land

## Characters
- **Ziggy** — round blue cartoon cat, yellow lightning bolt shirt
- **Bop** — round orange cartoon frog, rainbow shorts

## Visual Style
Flat bold cartoon illustration style. Thick outlines, bright saturated palette, no photorealism, no human faces or semi-realistic CGI.

## Backgrounds
- **Rainbow Meadow** — daytime outdoor meadow with giant colorful flowers, puffy clouds, rolling green hills
- **Zigzag Dance Stage** — bright indoor dance floor with zigzag tile pattern, colorful bunting flags, cartoon spotlight

## Song Structure
1. Intro (4 bars) — characters appear, instrument intro
2. Hook — "Zoomy zoom zoom... FREEZE!"
3. Verse 1 — Zoom and wiggle actions
4. Hook
5. Verse 2 — Slow-mo and spin actions
6. Hook
7. Bridge — Count-and-freeze (1, 2, 3, FREEZE!)
8. Final Hook
9. Outro — goodbye + one last freeze
