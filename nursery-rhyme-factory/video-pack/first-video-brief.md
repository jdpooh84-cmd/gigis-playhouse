## WHY THIS VIDEO FIRST

**Choice:** Sick Song | "What Should We Do?"  
**Not chosen:** Baby and the Bear Song

**Rationale:**
1. **Speed** — 2 characters (Gigi + Boo), 2 locations (bedroom, kitchen), no outdoor/animal rendering needed
2. **Emotional clarity** — problem/care/resolution arc is crystal-clear for toddlers; no ambiguity
3. **Universal appeal** — every child has been sick; every parent has comfort-cared; instant emotional hook
4. **Reusable structure** — the 3-beat arc (sick → care → better) is the most reusable template in the channel
5. **Search volume** — "sick baby song", "feel better song for kids", "caring nursery rhyme" are high-intent, low-competition long-tail terms
6. **Chorus repeatability** — a "What should we do?" call-and-response structure invites toddler participation, boosts watch time

---

## CONCEPT

**One sentence:** Gigi discovers her baby sibling Boo is sick, and the whole family sings about how to take care of someone who doesn't feel well.

**Scenario type:** sick_day  
**Emotional arc:** worried → caring → loving → relieved/happy  
**Educational arc:** what do we do when someone is sick? (empathy + basic care behaviors)

---

## LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Children learn 4 basic sick-care behaviors through song:
1. Rest in bed
2. Drink warm soup
3. Get a hug
4. Sleep and feel better

**Vocabulary introduced:** sick, sneeze, rest, soup, warm, hug, feel better, take care

---

## EMOTIONAL OBJECTIVE

Children experience that:
- It's okay to feel sick
- Someone who loves you will take care of you
- You will feel better
- Caring for others is loving

---

## PRODUCTION PRIORITIES

1. Boo's sick face must be expressive but not scary — slightly droopy eyes, red nose, hand over mouth
2. Gigi's "worried" face transitions to "determined" (I will help!) — this is the emotional center
3. Chorus visual must be energetic even though topic is "sick" — Gigi bouncing, asking "What should we do?"
4. Mama's appearance in bridge must feel warm and safe — low light, blanket scene
5. Final shot: Gigi and Boo laughing together — must be the thumbnail candidate
