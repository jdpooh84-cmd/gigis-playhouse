## LYRIC RULES

### Vocabulary
- Max word complexity: grade 1 (ages 5–6 reading level)
- Preferred words: simple, concrete, sensory
- Avoid: abstract concepts, complex emotions without visual anchor, words with 3+ syllables unless repeated throughout
- Test: can a 3-year-old repeat this line after one hearing?

### Line Length
- Max 10 syllables per lyric line
- Ideal: 6–8 syllables
- Count: "What should we do?" = 4 syllables ✓
- Count: "She tip-toed down the hall just right" = 8 syllables ✓

### Rhyme Scheme
- Verses: AABB (rhyme lines 1+2, lines 3+4)
- Chorus: ABAB or AABB — pick one and stick with it per song
- Bridge: ABAB, slower — can be softer rhyme
- Never force rhyme at the cost of clarity

### Chorus Rules
- Must appear minimum 3× per song
- Must be singable in isolation (child can learn it first)
- Must have a call-and-response structure OR a repeated hook phrase
- Max 8 lines
- Final chorus should differ slightly — past tense or "we did it" variation

### Character Dialogue
- Use quotation lines sparingly — max 2–3 per verse
- Dialogue should be 4–6 words max
- Always attribute: "'[words],' said [character]"

### Sound Effects
- "Achoo!" "Splash!" "Clap clap clap!" work well
- Place at the start of a line for emphasis
- Should be physically performable by toddler

## EMOTIONAL ARC CHECKLIST

Every song must hit these emotional marks in order:

| Beat | When | How |
|------|------|-----|
| Establish normal | Intro/Verse 1 | Character in familiar setting |
| Introduce disruption | Verse 1 midpoint | Problem appears |
| Emotional response | End Verse 1 | Character reacts (worried/curious/excited) |
| Action taken | Verse 2 | Concrete help behaviors |
| Comfort/care | Bridge | Slower, tender moment |
| Shift toward resolution | Verse 3 | Signs of improvement |
| Resolution | Final Chorus | Problem solved, joy expressed |
| Universal lesson | Outro | Generalized takeaway for viewer |
