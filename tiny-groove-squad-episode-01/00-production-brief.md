# Tiny Groove Squad — Episode 1 Production Brief

## Show
**Title:** Tiny Groove Squad
**Target Age:** 2–6
**Core Theme:** Kids use music, movement, and small acts of courage to turn everyday moments into adventures.
**Pillars:** Music and dance · Everyday adventures · Feelings and friendship · Safety and routines (light touch) · Simple imagination grounded in reality

## Episode 1
**Episode Type:** Pure dance/action song — no story beat, no adult anchor
**Runtime Target:** 2–4 minutes
**Tone:** Warm, bright, energetic but not hyper
**Hook Phrase:** "Everybody groove!"

## Move Structure
| Section | Move Pair |
|---------|-----------|
| Verse 1 | Jump + Clap |
| Verse 2 | Spin + Stomp |
| Verse 3 | Wiggle + Freeze |

## Primary Location
The Groove Room — bright playroom, rainbow rug center stage, string lights, toy speaker. Warm yellow walls. Floor cushions. No clutter.

## Technical Requirements
- Full-body shots for every move
- Each move held minimum 4 seconds
- Bright, non-flashing colors only
- No bath, potty, hygiene, woodland, fantasy, or adult-brand content
- No story beat — pure dance/action
