# Tiny Groove Squad — Episode 1: Music Direction

## Style
- Genre: Upbeat children's pop / kids dance song
- Feel: Warm, bright, energetic but NOT hyper
- Tempo: Moderate-upbeat — around 100–110 BPM. Fast enough to dance, slow enough for a 3-year-old to follow
- Key: Major key only — bright and positive
- Instrumentation: Simple, clean. Prioritize beat (drum/clap track), a melodic hook, and one bass element
- No synth-heavy or EDM drops — keep it organic and warm

## Structure
```
Intro          4 bars     — instrumental, beat drops, energy builds
Chorus 1       8 bars     — full vocals
Verse 1        8 bars     — full vocals (Jump + Clap)
Chorus 2       8 bars     — full vocals
Verse 2        8 bars     — full vocals (Spin + Stomp)
Chorus 3       8 bars     — full vocals
Verse 3        8 bars     — full vocals (Wiggle + Freeze)
Outro x2       8+8 bars   — full vocals, second pass slightly softer, fade
```

## Signature Sound Elements
- **Clap track:** Audible handclap sound on beats 2 and 4 — kids can clap along naturally
- **Stomp hit:** A low thud or bass thump on the stomp beats in Verse 2
- **Freeze cue:** A brief musical "sting" or sudden silence on the word "FREEZE" in Verse 3 — makes the freeze moment playful and obvious
- **Outro fade:** Gradual fade over the final 8 bars — music and vocals fade together

## What to Avoid
- No minor key sections
- No jarring sound effects or sudden loud bursts
- No flashing or strobing audio edit effects
- No adult/pop music references
- No lyrics referencing night, sleep, bath, potty, or fantasy elements

## Lyric Delivery Notes
- Cheerful, clear, easy to sing along with
- Slightly slow the lyric phrasing so young children can follow
- On "FREEZE" — deliver with a fun, exaggerated pause before the word, then shout it with a grin
- On "Everybody GROOVE!" — the final word hits on the downbeat, punchy

## Sync Points for Animation
| Beat | Music Cue | Visual Action |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Intro bar 1 | Beat drops | String lights flicker on |
| Intro bar 3 | Energy builds | Kids run onto rug |
| Verse 1 lyric "Jump up high" | Strong downbeat | Jump starts |
| Verse 2 lyric "Spin around" | Spin cue | Spin starts |
| Verse 3 lyric "Now FREEZE" | Sudden music sting or silence | Freeze happens |
| Verse 3 lyric "wiggle back" | Beat resumes | Wiggle burst resumes |
| Outro final bar | Fade begins | Kids wave, slow to final pose |
