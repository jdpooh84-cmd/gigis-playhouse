# Tiny Groove Squad — Episode 1: Storyboard Shot List

## Framing Rules
- All move shots: FULL-BODY frame (head to feet visible)
- Every move held minimum 4 seconds before cut
- No flashing edits or strobe cuts
- Bright, non-flashing colors at all times
- Camera cuts only on beat, never mid-move-hold
