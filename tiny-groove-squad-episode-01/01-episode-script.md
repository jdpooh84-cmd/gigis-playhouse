# Tiny Groove Squad — Episode 1: "Everybody Groove!"
## Full Script and Lyrics

---

### FORMAT NOTES
- Episode type: Pure dance/action song — no story beat, no dialogue, no adult anchor
- Runtime: 2–4 minutes
- All lyric lines: 6–8 words, simple rhyme pairs, direct action verbs only
- Chorus hook phrase: "Everybody groove!"

---

## STRUCTURE

```
INTRO (instrumental, 4 bars)
CHORUS 1
VERSE 1 — Jump + Clap
CHORUS 2
VERSE 2 — Spin + Stomp
CHORUS 3
VERSE 3 — Wiggle + Freeze
OUTRO CHORUS (x2, fade)
```

---

## FULL SCRIPT

### [INTRO — 4 bars instrumental]
*String lights flicker on. The Groove Room comes alive.*
*Max, Lily, Theo, Nina, and Sam run onto the rainbow rug.*
*They face the camera, big smiles, ready to go.*

---

### [CHORUS 1]

> **Everybody groove! Everybody move!**
> **Come and feel the beat with us!**
> **Everybody groove! Let's go go go!**
> **Everybody GROOVE!**

*All five kids bounce in place on the rainbow rug.*
*Wide smiles, arms loose at sides, bodies swaying.*

---

### [VERSE 1 — Jump + Clap]

**[On-screen cue card: JUMP + CLAP]**

> **Jump up high and clap your hands!**
> **Feel the beat across the land!**
> **Clap clap clap and jump jump jump!**
> **Get the groove with every thump!**

*Move breakdown:*
- *Bar 1–2: All five jump — feet leave ground, land soft. Arms out wide. (Hold 4 sec)*
- *Bar 3–4: All five clap hands overhead three times. (Hold 4 sec)*
- *Bar 5–6: Alternate: jump, clap, jump, clap. Repeat.*
- *Max leads — wide-stance jump, arms out. Others mirror.*
- *Sam stomps both fists at hip height with open-mouth grin between jumps.*

---

### [CHORUS 2]

> **Everybody groove! Everybody move!**
> **Come and feel the beat with us!**
> **Everybody groove! Let's go go go!**
> **Everybody GROOVE!**

*Group bounces on rug, each kid showing their signature energy.*

---

### [VERSE 2 — Spin + Stomp]

**[On-screen cue card: SPIN + STOMP]**

> **Spin around and stomp the floor!**
> **Spin and stomp, we want some more!**
> **Stomp stomp stomp your feet so loud!**
> **Spin around and show the crowd!**

*Move breakdown:*
- *Bar 1–2: All five spin in place — one full slow turn. Arms out. (Hold spin 4 sec)*
- *Bar 3–4: All five stomp feet alternating left-right. Big stomp. (Hold 4 sec)*
- *Bar 5–6: Spin then stomp, spin then stomp. Repeat.*
- *Lily leads spin — her slow spin into twirl.*
- *Theo leads stomp — stiff robot arms, wide grin, exaggerated stomp.*
- *Nina punctuates stomp with her sharp double-clap and nod.*

---

### [CHORUS 3]

> **Everybody groove! Everybody move!**
> **Come and feel the beat with us!**
> **Everybody groove! Let's go go go!**
> **Everybody GROOVE!**

*Kids high-five each other mid-chorus. Energy building.*

---

### [VERSE 3 — Wiggle + Freeze]

**[On-screen cue card: WIGGLE + FREEZE]**

> **Wiggle wiggle from your head to toe!**
> **Wiggle fast now, don't be slow!**
> **Now FREEZE — hold still and don't you move!**
> **Then wiggle back into the groove!**

*Move breakdown:*
- *Bar 1–2: All five wiggle full body — head, shoulders, hips, legs, feet. (Hold 4 sec)*
- *Bar 3: Freeze! All five stop completely, wide eyes, hold position. (Hold 4 sec)*
- *Bar 4: Burst back into wiggle with big laugh/grin.*
- *Sam leads the freeze — fists at hip height, then sudden wide-eyed full stop.*
- *Theo exaggerates the freeze with his robot-arm pose locked mid-motion.*

---

### [OUTRO CHORUS — x2, fade]

> **Everybody groove! Everybody move!**
> **Come and feel the beat with us!**
> **Everybody groove! Let's go go go!**
> **Everybody GROOVE!**

*(Repeat once more, slightly slower, voices fade with music.)*

*Kids wave at camera. Big smiles. Lights dim slowly on Groove Room.*
*Final frame: all five on rainbow rug, arms around each other.*

---

## END OF EPISODE 1
