# Tiny Groove Squad — Episode 1: Voice Direction

## Overview
Episode 1 is a pure dance/action song. Vocals are sung, not spoken. No dialogue, no narration, no adult voices.

---

## Vocal Style
- **Style:** Sung, group, children's pop
- **Energy:** Bright, warm, genuinely joyful — not performed or overdone
- **Pitch:** Natural child register — do not pitch-shift down to adult or up to cartoon squeak
- **Diction:** Clear and clean — every word understood by a 2-year-old
- **Group singing:** All five voices together on chorus. Slight variation on verses (one voice leads, others join)

---

## Chorus Delivery ("Everybody groove!")
- Full group voice
- Last word "GROOVE" punches on the downbeat — held 1 beat
- Bright and inviting, like calling a friend over
- Not a shout — warm shout, like playing in a park

---

## Verse Delivery

### Verse 1 — Jump + Clap
- Lyric phrasing bounces with the jump rhythm
- "Clap clap clap" — three clear syllables with natural clap energy
- "Jump jump jump" — playful, the word itself bounces

### Verse 2 — Spin + Stomp
- "Stomp stomp stomp" — heavier, grounded — not angry, just solid and fun
- "Spin around" — lighter, airy before the stomp lands

### Verse 3 — Wiggle + Freeze
- "Wiggle wiggle" — loose, silly, undulating delivery
- **"Now FREEZE"** — slight pause before "FREEZE," then deliver with fun dramatic clarity. Like shouting "Surprise!" — delighted, not startled.
- "Then wiggle back" — immediate return to silly loose energy

---

## Tone Notes
- No whining, no tension, no anticipatory dread
- The freeze is fun, not scary
- The whole track should feel like playing with a best friend

---

## Voice Generation Notes (Higgsfield TTS)
- Use `seed_audio` model
- Select a voice preset appropriate for a bright, warm, child-friendly children's song lead
- Deliver chorus as a single clip
- Deliver each verse as its own clip
- All lyric lines must be 6–8 words, direct action verbs, simple rhyme pairs (as written in 01-episode-script.md)
- Do not ad-lib or add words not in the script
