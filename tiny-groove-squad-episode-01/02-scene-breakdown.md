## Scene 1 — INTRO
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**Action:** String lights flicker on. Groove Room is empty for 2 seconds, then all five kids run onto the rainbow rug from the sides. They face the camera. Big smiles, bodies bouncing lightly.
**Camera:** Wide shot — full Groove Room visible. Rainbow rug center frame. String lights in background. Toy speaker on left side.
**Mood:** Anticipation → energy burst
**No dialogue. No narration. Music only.**

---

## Scene 2 — CHORUS 1
**Duration:** ~20 seconds
**Action:** All five kids bounce on the rainbow rug in a loose group. Arms loose. Shoulders bouncing. Sam at front-center. Max at back-left. Lily at back-right. Theo at front-right. Nina at front-left.
**Camera:** Full-body wide shot of all five. Cut to close-up of Sam's big grin. Cut back to wide.
**Mood:** Bright, welcoming, joyful

---

## Scene 3 — VERSE 1: JUMP + CLAP
**Duration:** ~30 seconds
**Beat breakdown:**
- **Beat 1 (4 sec):** All five jump — feet leave ground, soft landing, arms out wide. Max leads from center. Full-body shot.
- **Beat 2 (4 sec):** All five clap hands overhead three times. Big arm movement.
- **Beat 3–4 (remaining):** Alternating jump → clap → jump → clap. Repeat to lyric end.
**Camera:** Wide full-body. Cut to Max (wide-stance jump, arms out). Cut back to wide. Cut to Sam (fists at hips, grin). Back to wide.
**On-screen cue card:** "JUMP + CLAP" — displayed 2 seconds before move starts, white rounded card, friendly font, warm yellow or orange background chip.

---

## Scene 4 — CHORUS 2
**Duration:** ~20 seconds
**Action:** All five return to bounce in place. High energy. Smiles wide. Natural body movement. No choreographed move — just feeling the music.
**Camera:** Wide shot. One mid-shot of Theo's robot arms bouncing.

---

## Scene 5 — VERSE 2: SPIN + STOMP
**Duration:** ~30 seconds
**Beat breakdown:**
- **Beat 1 (4 sec):** All five spin in place — one full slow turn, arms out. Lily leads — slow spin into twirl.
- **Beat 2 (4 sec):** All five stomp feet alternating left-right. Big exaggerated stomp. Theo leads with stiff robot arms, wide grin.
- **Beat 3–4 (remaining):** Spin → stomp → spin → stomp. Repeat.
**Camera:** Wide full-body. Cut to Lily mid-spin. Cut to Theo's exaggerated stomp. Cut to Nina's double-clap nod. Back to wide.
**On-screen cue card:** "SPIN + STOMP"

---

## Scene 6 — CHORUS 3
**Duration:** ~20 seconds
**Action:** Energy peaks. Kids high-five each other — two quick pairs then all five in a circle. Sam reaches up for high-five, others reach down to meet Sam. Big laughs, bouncing.
**Camera:** Wide shot, then close-up of the center high-five circle.

---

## Scene 7 — VERSE 3: WIGGLE + FREEZE
**Duration:** ~30 seconds
**Beat breakdown:**
- **Beat 1 (4 sec):** All five wiggle full body — head, shoulders, hips, legs, feet. Loose and silly. Sam leads from front.
- **Beat 2 (4 sec):** All five FREEZE — complete stop, wide eyes, hold position mid-wiggle. Theo frozen in robot pose.
- **Beat 3 (4 sec):** Burst back to wiggle — big laugh energy.
- **Beat 4 (remaining):** Wiggle → freeze → wiggle pattern repeats.
**Camera:** Wide full-body. Close-up on the freeze moment — Theo's frozen robot arms. Sam's wide-eyed freeze. Back to wide for the wiggle return burst.
**On-screen cue card:** "WIGGLE + FREEZE"

---

## Scene 8 — OUTRO CHORUS x2
**Duration:** ~35 seconds
**Action:** Two full chorus runs. First pass: full energy, all bouncing. Second pass: energy settles, kids wave at camera between phrases. Final phrase: all five together on rainbow rug, arms over each other's shoulders or around each other's waists. Big smiles at camera.
**Camera:** Wide shot → slow push-in as energy settles → final frame: all five, rainbow rug, string lights glowing behind them.
**Lights:** String lights glow warm as music fades.
**End frame holds 3 seconds before cut to black.**
