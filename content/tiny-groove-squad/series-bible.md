# Tiny Groove Squad — Series Bible

---

## 1. Series Description

### For Parents

Tiny Groove Squad is a short-form dance and music series for children ages 2–6. Each episode is 2–4 minutes long and built around one simple idea, one catchy chorus, and a small set of body movements that children can copy at home. The cast is five ordinary kids — Max, Lily, Theo, Nina, and Sam — who dance together in real, recognizable places: a playroom, a park, a classroom, and a backyard. There is no fantasy world, no brand universe, and no adult pop-culture references. Every song is written to be repeated, shouted, and danced to by little kids, and every episode ends with a clear invitation to watch again. Parents will find the content calm enough to tolerate, energetic enough to actually get kids moving, and short enough to fit into any part of the day.

### For Kids (read aloud or shown on screen)

Hey! We're the Tiny Groove Squad — and we LOVE to dance!
Every time you see us, we're going to learn brand-new moves together.
You can jump, clap, spin, stomp, wiggle, and FREEZE!
It doesn't matter if you get it perfect. Just get up and move with us!
Ready? Let's GROOVE!

## 2. Cast Description

> Use this section as the primary reference for character design, color palettes, and animation style. All characters are ordinary children in everyday clothing. No fantasy features, wings, glowing eyes, or animal ears.

---

### MAX
- **Age:** 5
- **Role:** The leader and first mover. Max shows each new move before anyone else, then looks at the camera and invites viewers to try.
- **Signature moves:** Big two-footed jumps, wide-arm victory poses, arms-up "come on!" gesture.
- **Personality:** Brave, friendly, and a little too fast. He sometimes launches into a move before the music starts and has to reset — which makes him relatable rather than perfect.
- **Design notes:** Medium-warm skin tone. Short, natural curly hair, tight and close to the head. Wears a bright yellow t-shirt (his color) and dark blue shorts. White sneakers with a yellow stripe. Always looks energized — wide stance, slight lean forward when ready.

---

### LILY
- **Age:** 4
- **Role:** The shy-but-brave one. Lily watches Max first, takes a breath, and then joins in. She models the emotional arc of "unsure → trying → proud."
- **Signature moves:** Slow, graceful spins with arms out, controlled twirls with one foot lifted.
- **Personality:** Creative, thoughtful, quiet at first. She notices details others miss and often finds the prettiest version of a move.
- **Design notes:** Light warm skin tone with faint freckles across the nose. Long straight hair in two loose braids, one tied with a purple ribbon. Wears a lavender long-sleeve shirt (her color) and light gray leggings. Purple canvas shoes. Her face often shows a small, careful smile — she's thinking before she commits.

---

### THEO
- **Age:** 6
- **Role:** The comedy engine. Theo keeps the energy high and makes kids laugh, but he also has to learn to control his silliness (especially for the Freeze move).
- **Signature moves:** Stiff robot arms, exaggerated shrug, full-body wriggle, funny freeze faces.
- **Personality:** Goofy, high-energy, sometimes too wiggly. He genuinely tries to help but ends up going too far — and learning from it.
- **Design notes:** Medium-dark warm skin tone. Short twists/locs, neatly shaped. Wears an orange long-sleeve shirt (his color) and bright green shorts — intentionally a bit mismatched. Bright orange sneakers. His expression is almost always a big open grin; his eyebrows do a lot of work.

---

### NINA
- **Age:** 5
- **Role:** The organizer. Nina counts the steps out loud, keeps the group in sync, and reminds everyone where to stand. Her arc across episodes is learning that fun matters as much as precision.
- **Signature moves:** Sharp double-claps, counted step-hops (one-two-three-step), neat side-to-side rock.
- **Personality:** Responsible, focused, occasionally bossy. She cares deeply about doing it right and is genuinely surprised when the "wrong" version is still fun.
- **Design notes:** Deep warm brown skin tone. Natural hair in a puff with a teal hair tie. Wears a teal t-shirt (her color) and navy blue shorts. White sneakers with teal laces. Posture is upright, hands often at ready position. Her face defaults to focused concentration; her smile is a reward the viewer earns.

---

### SAM
- **Age:** 3
- **Role:** The heart of the show. Sam is the youngest and the most purely joyful. Sam's job is to show that even the smallest kid can dance — imperfectly and happily.
- **Signature moves:** Heavy two-footed stomps, full-body wiggle (whole body shakes top to bottom), arms-up celebration.
- **Personality:** Excited about everything, occasionally overwhelmed by big or fast moves. When things get too fast, Sam slows down and finds a simpler version — modeling self-regulation for the youngest viewers.
- **Design notes:** Light medium skin tone. Short wispy hair, slightly messy. Wears a bright red t-shirt (their color) and denim shorts with rolled cuffs. Red and white striped socks visible above small white sneakers. Sam is notably shorter than the other kids — this size difference is important and should be consistent. Expression is almost always wide-eyed delight.

---

### MISS JOY (Optional Adult)
- **Role:** Background support figure — teacher or parent-adjacent. She does not lead or teach; she facilitates.
- **Functions:** Occasionally presses play on the speaker, reminds kids to "check your space" before a big move, and appears briefly in outros or between-episode transitions.
- **Design notes:** Adult woman, warm medium skin tone, natural hair pulled back loosely. Wears a solid sage green top and simple dark pants — calm, uncluttered. Her clothing color never competes with the kids' bright palette. Always positioned at the edge of the frame; never center-stage.

## 3. World Description

> Use this section as the primary reference for background design, set dressing, and scene layout. All locations are real, recognizable places. No magic, no glowing effects, no impossible architecture.

---

### THE GROOVE ROOM (Primary Location)
**What it is:** A bright indoor playroom that functions as the Squad's "home base" for dancing.

**Key elements (always present):**
- A round rug in warm colors (orange, yellow, coral) that serves as the "stage." The rug has a simple geometric pattern — no characters, no logos.
- A small wireless Bluetooth speaker on a low shelf. This is what plays the music. It is white with a small colored stripe. Miss Joy sometimes touches this.
- Three or four large floor cushions in solid colors (matching the kids' palette: yellow, lavender, orange, teal, red) pushed to the edges when dancing.
- One low shelf with a few simple objects: a tambourine, a small drum, some colorful balls. These are props; they are not always used.
- Large windows with soft natural light on one side. Curtains are white with a simple yellow hem.
- Walls are off-white. One wall has a hand-painted stripe at kid height in the five character colors: yellow, lavender, orange, teal, red.

**What it must NOT have:** Clutter, dark corners, complicated murals, fantasy imagery, or screen/TV props.

**Camera feel:** Warm, airy, slightly wide. The room always feels big enough to dance in.

---

### SUNBEAM PARK (Secondary Location)
**What it is:** A neighborhood park used for outdoor dance episodes.

**Key elements (always present):**
- A small rectangular wooden platform (about 8 inches high, 10 feet wide) that serves as the outdoor "stage." It is painted a warm weather-worn yellow.
- Green grass surrounds the platform. A clear blue sky with a few simple clouds is always visible.
- One wooden park bench to one side (where Miss Joy can sit if present).
- A few simple trees in the background — round canopy, solid green, not detailed. These are background elements only.
- Warm natural daylight — always feels like mid-morning on a sunny day.

**What it must NOT have:** Playground equipment visible in dance shots (it distracts), other children in the background, fantasy elements, or dramatic lighting.

**Camera feel:** Slightly brighter and more open than the Groove Room. Wider shots feel natural here.

---

### BRIGHT BEGINNINGS ROOM (Tertiary Location)
**What it is:** A classroom or daycare corner — for group participation episodes.

**Key elements:** Low tables pushed to the walls, a small circle of tape on the floor where kids stand, colorful alphabet and number posters on the walls (legible but not dominant), cubbies in the background.

---

### BACKYARD BASH (Special Episodes)
**What it is:** A backyard setup for party or celebration episodes.

**Key elements:** Simple string lights (off during day scenes, on during evening scenes), a folding table with healthy snacks, some colorful bunting, a small portable speaker.

## 4. Episode Format Checklist

> Copy this checklist for every new episode. Every box must be checked before an episode is considered complete.

---

### Structure Checklist

- [ ] **Total runtime:** 2–4 minutes
- [ ] **Intro (10–15 sec):** Kids address camera, say the show name, invite viewers to dance
- [ ] **Setup (10–20 sec):** One simple sentence of context (where they are, what they're doing)
- [ ] **Main song (90–150 sec):** Verses + chorus, built around clear body moves
- [ ] **Outro (10–20 sec):** Short goodbye, invitation to replay or watch again

---

### Song Rules Checklist

- [ ] One main chorus hook (6–8 words max, easily shouted by a 3-year-old)
- [ ] Chorus repeats at least 3 times across the episode
- [ ] No more than 3 verses
- [ ] Each verse introduces 1–2 moves only
- [ ] All lyrics use simple words (6–8 words per line)
- [ ] Every named move is one of: jump, clap, spin, stomp, wiggle, freeze, step, sway, shake
- [ ] Tempo is upbeat but not frantic (target BPM: 100–120)
- [ ] No adult references, brand names, or fantasy world vocabulary

---

### Visual Rules Checklist

- [ ] At least one clear full-body shot showing each move
- [ ] Each move shot held for 4+ seconds so a child can see and copy
- [ ] Colors are bright but gentle (no neon extremes, no dark scenes)
- [ ] No flashing, strobing, or rapid-cut editing
- [ ] Faces clearly readable — emotions obvious at a glance
- [ ] Bodies fully visible during moves — no cropping at the waist during a jump or stomp
- [ ] Camera moves slowly or holds still; no shaky-cam or snap-zoom

---

### Character Checklist

- [ ] Max is established as the leader in the intro
- [ ] At least one other squad member has a featured moment
- [ ] Sam (the youngest) is visible and participating — even if doing a simpler version
- [ ] No character is absent for more than 30 consecutive seconds

---

### Brand Safety Checklist

- [ ] No fantasy worlds, glowing creatures, or magic systems
- [ ] No references to other shows, brands, or properties
- [ ] No adult humor or double meanings
- [ ] Miss Joy (if present) is support-only — never leads, never lectures
- [ ] All locations match the approved world list

## 5. Episode 1 — Full Package

---

### Concept Summary

**Title:** Get Up and Groove

**Tagline:** "Jump, spin, wiggle, FREEZE!"

**Core idea:** The very first Tiny Groove Squad episode. Max leads the squad through three simple two-move combos — jump+clap, spin+stomp, wiggle+freeze — in the Groove Room. The goal is to get kids on their feet immediately, establish the format, and give them a chorus they'll be shouting by the second repeat.

**Location:** The Groove Room
**Lead character:** Max
**Supporting:** Lily, Theo, Nina, Sam
**Miss Joy:** Visible briefly at the start (she turns on the speaker) and at the end (she smiles and nods from the doorway).

---

### Full Script

---

**[INTRO — 12 seconds]**

*The five kids stand on the round rug in the Groove Room. They face the camera. Max steps forward.*

**MAX:** Hey! We're the Tiny Groove Squad!
**ALL FIVE:** *(together)* And it's time to DANCE!
**MAX:** *(waving toward camera)* Come on — dance with us!

---

**[SETUP — 15 seconds]**

*Miss Joy walks to the speaker shelf at the edge of the room. She presses play. A soft beat starts.*

**NINA:** *(claps twice)* Okay Squad — let's get our groove on!
**SAM:** *(bouncing in place)* I'm ready! I'm ready! I'm ready!
**THEO:** *(robot arms already going)* Me too! Me too! Me too!
**LILY:** *(taking a breath, small smile)* Okay. Let's go.

*Max faces front. The beat picks up. The song begins.*

---

**[MAIN SONG — see Full Lyrics below]**

---

**[OUTRO — 15 seconds]**

*The music winds down. All five kids are slightly out of breath, smiling. Sam is still wiggling. They face the camera.*

**MAX:** That's the Tiny Groove Squad!
**NINA:** *(counting on fingers)* Jump — clap — spin — stomp — wiggle — freeze!
**THEO:** *(frozen in a silly pose)* Still… frozen… *(then breaks)* Just kidding!
**LILY:** *(warmly)* You did so great.
**SAM:** *(arms up)* AGAIN! AGAIN!
**MAX:** *(to camera)* You want to do it again? Press replay!
**ALL FIVE:** *(together)* Byeeee, Groove Squad!

> Spin and stomp — you've got the beat!

- Shot 23: Lily steps forward, medium shot. She takes a breath and opens her arms.

  - *Note: camera must hold the full spin; do not cut mid-rotation.*
- Shot 25: Full-body shot — Lily's stomp, foot raised and planted firmly. Hold 4 seconds.
- Shot 26: Wide shot — Sam watches, then does a big double-footed stomp. Sam's version is simpler and that's fine; show it clearly.

---

**Scene 7 — Chorus (third) (1:43–1:58)**
- Shot 29: Wide shot — all five do full sequence. Energy peaks here.

---

**Scene 8 — Verse 3: Wiggle + Freeze (1:58–2:22)**
- Shot 30: Theo steps forward. He immediately starts wiggling — too wiggly, whole body flailing.
- Shot 31: Full-body shot — Theo's maximum wiggle. Head, shoulders, hips, knees all moving. Hold 5 seconds.
- Shot 32: Medium shot on Nina watching Theo, laughing despite herself.
- Shot 33: Full-body shot — FREEZE. Theo freezes mid-wiggle in a ridiculous position. Hold 5 seconds. *This is a comedy beat.*
- Shot 34: Wide shot — all five do the wiggle together, each in their own style.
  - Max: big, athletic wiggle.
  - Lily: gentle, flowing wiggle.
  - Theo: maximum chaos.
  - Nina: precise, controlled wiggle (her version of wild).
  - Sam: full-body shake, falls over laughing, gets back up.
- Shot 35: Wide shot — all five FREEZE together. Hold 4 seconds. Camera holds. Complete silence.

---

**Scene 9 — Final Chorus (2:22–2:45)**
- Shot 36: Wide shot — all five do the full chorus sequence, building energy.
- Shot 37: On the final "FREEZE!" — all five strike big individual poses and hold.
  - Max: arms-up victory pose, wide stance.
  - Lily: one arm up, one out, one leg slightly lifted — a dancer's freeze.
  - Theo: robot arms, eyes crossed, tongue out.
  - Nina: both arms out at sharp angles, perfect balance.
  - Sam: both arms straight up, biggest grin, slightly tipping sideways.
- Shot 38: Hold the freeze. 4 seconds of silence. Then all five break at once and laugh.

---

**Scene 10 — Outro (2:45–3:00)**
- Shot 39: Wide shot — all five slightly out of breath, smiling, facing camera.
- Shot 40: Medium shot on Nina counting moves on her fingers.
- Shot 41: Close-up on Theo still frozen in silly pose, then breaking.
- Shot 42: Medium shot on Lily: "You did so great."
- Shot 43: Close-up on Sam: arms up — "AGAIN! AGAIN!"
- Shot 44: Medium shot on Max to camera: "You want to do it again? Press replay!"
- Shot 45: Wide shot — all five wave: "Byeeee, Groove Squad!"
- Shot 46: Wide shot holds 2 seconds. Music fades. Miss Joy visible in the doorway, smiling.
