## E. SUPERVISOR — FIRST CONFLICT & FLAG REPORT

*Per the rules, the Supervisor does not resolve these; it surfaces them to the Showrunners with a recommendation. Five items.*

**CONFLICT 1 — Co-parenting canon contradicts itself across documents.**
- Doc 5 / Agent 08: *"Daniel's parents… co-parent WELL… celebrates healthy co-parenting, not divorce."* (LOCKED)
- Doc 2 heritage canon + my Part 2/4 reconciliation: an *intact, united bicultural household* (married parents), which I defaulted to because Part 2/3 never restated separation.
- **These cannot both be silently true.** *Recommendation:* Showrunners pick one — **(a)** co-parenting across two homes (restores the original Part 1 pillar; richer for blended-family audiences), or **(b)** united bicultural household (simpler; matches the heritage-reveal framing). Either works; the film just must commit. *(My lean: (a) — it's rarer on screen and serves "belonging" more, but this is your call with Rachel.)*

**CONFLICT 2 — Where does the betrayal happen: local or regionals?**
- Agent 01 Beat 1: *"wrong choice at **regionals**. Loses first crew."*
- Agent 02 / Part 3 Level structure: betrayal at **Level One (local/school)**; the final must be *earned back* from there.
- **Recommendation:** adopt **Level One (local/school)** — it gives Daniel the longest climb back and matches the three-level ladder. Agent 01's "regionals" should be corrected to "local." Showrunner confirm.

**FLAG 3 (Moral-physics) — the faked injury (new Act-3 mechanism). — RESOLVED.**
- "Main dancer fakes an injury so Daniel must step forward" risks reading as a *deception* rewarded — inside a film whose physics punish self-interest and honor truth. The first fix proposed (a "this is yours" hand-off) was rejected by the Showrunner as overplayed — it makes the moment about the giver's nobility and renders Daniel passive.
- **RESOLUTION (Showrunner-directed, locked as proposal):** replaced with **"The Choice" beat** — a **real injury** (the Fracture's attack on the crew's lead, so it's honest *and* raises supernatural stakes) and then the drama is **recognition, not generosity**: Coach Wren chooses Daniel out of the whole cast *with certainty* ("I'm not guessing. I saw it.") because she secretly witnessed his true self dance earlier. Pays off Daniel's exact wound (never picked → chosen first by the one who sees him); he still must earn it via the praise dance; the Fracture's division-move backfires by forcing forward the one dancer who completes the Circle. Full development: **`content/dance-competition-the-choice-beat.md`**. Supervisor checklist: GREEN across theme, moral physics, character, narrative, north star.

**FLAG 4 (Representation/Moral-physics) — carried, still top priority.**
- **Abilities-vs-diagnosis (O12):** Doc 5 Agent 04 restates "chosen because of how their minds work." Without the Part 2 decoupling (gift is *bloodline*, neurotype is *access*, ≥1 neurotypical carrier), this still reads as magical-disability. *Recommendation: lock the decoupling; route through a neurodiversity consultant with story authority.*
- **TÌ's paralysis:** must be the **Fracture's grip that releases**, not permanent injury — or it punishes a child (violates "no purely punitive ending"). Agent 06's guardrail ("shown through absence of movement, his face does the work, not injury") helps; add that the grip **lifts** when the Fracture fractures. *Recommendation: lock "temporary grip," confirm with the child-psychology lens.*

**FLAG 5 (Legal) — "LEGALLY CLEAR" is overstated; treat as preliminary.**
- Agent 11 marks the Shadow Iguanas and the restaurant "LEGALLY CLEAR — confirmed via Apify research." **Web research is not legal clearance.** Two specific exposures remain: (a) the restaurant is *engineered to be immediately recognizable* as a specific chain (signature sauce + lemonade + lines-around-the-block) — that's **trade-dress** territory, and the "First Circle Combo" tie-in adds **false-endorsement** risk; (b) homemade *parody-adjacent* costumes are usually fine as a category, but "immediately recognizable" is exactly the line trademark/trade-dress polices.
- **Recommendation:** downgrade both to **YELLOW (clear to develop, not to produce)** pending review by **actual IP counsel.** Keep the creative; don't let a self-issued "green" travel into production. (This is the Supervisor refusing to rubber-stamp — the system working as designed.)

## F. HONEST NOTE ON THE SYSTEM ITSELF

The hierarchy is genuinely strong — it turns the vision into checkable gates and a single source of truth, which is exactly what a sprawling, five-document universe needs. Two cautions, offered plainly:
1. **Don't let the process outrun the art.** Eleven agents + a Canon Log + per-scene QA is heavyweight. For a project still in *development*, run it **lightweight**: use the Canon Log and the Supervisor conflict-pass (this document), but don't gate every brainstorm through eleven reports or you'll bury the spark the film is about. Scale the ceremony up at pre-production, not now.
2. **The system can't manufacture the human authorities it depends on.** Consultants (cultural, neurodiversity, child-psych), a composer, and IP counsel are load-bearing in this brief. The agents marked "Needs human authority" in §D are **blocked** until those people exist — flagging that honestly so the Canon Log never reads "cleared" when it means "AI-checked."
