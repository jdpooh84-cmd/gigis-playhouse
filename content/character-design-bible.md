# THE FIRST CIRCLE — CHARACTER DESIGN BIBLE

*The visual + aesthetic build for every major character — enough for a character designer (or an image tool) to actually create them. Each entry covers build & proportions, face, hair, color palette, shape language, silhouette, wardrobe (everyday + performance, start vs. end), body language, and "personality in the design." A copy-pasteable concept-art prompt closes each lead. Consistent with the locked canon and the Part 1/Part 4 visual system.*

---

## 0. THE VISUAL SYSTEM (read first — it governs every design)

**Rendering style:** stylized 3D CG with a hand-crafted warmth — appealing, rounded, expressive faces with real skin and fabric texture, but pushed and graphic, not photoreal. The signature that makes it *ours*: **the frame is choreographed to rhythm** — editing, light, and even hair/cloth simulation pulse subtly to the music. Animation is the reason this story is animated: it can show what's happening *inside* Daniel and Stella, which live-action can't.

**The color-coding logic (consistent everywhere):**
- **Warmth / safety / belonging:** saturated, warm light, golden-hour glow, color that "spills." Home, the cypher, the Undercommons, Mamá Àbèní.
- **The Fracture's presence:** color drains toward grey-violet; edges go slightly wrong; rhythmic light stutters. Not black horror — *desaturation and arrhythmia.*
- **Each House has a palette** (below). Daniel = warm/syncopated; TÌ = gold + obsidian, flawless and cold.
- **The First Circle restoring:** all the House palettes bloom back in at once, distinct but harmonized.

**Shape-language philosophy:**
- **Rounder shapes = openness, warmth, safety** (Daniel, Nia, Theo, Mamá Àbèní, Bo-type kids).
- **Sharper, more controlled shapes = guardedness, perfectionism, the system** (TÌ, House Malhi, the rubric world).
- **Every major character must read in silhouette alone** — one shape you'd know in pure shadow.

**Two bespoke "inner-world" visual languages (handled with dignity, never clinical):**
- **Daniel's rhythm-sense:** when his gift stirs, the world briefly gains *motion-trails and anticipatory beats* — faint after-images showing where things are about to move. Warm, musical, cool — never a symptom.
- **Stella's pattern-sight:** the world briefly *gridlines and tessellates* into beautiful geometry — lines connecting people, stress-points glowing where a pattern will break. Elegant, not a diagnosis. We hear/see as she does for a few seconds, then return.

**Costume tells the arc (rule):** Daniel's first-scene look and his finale look should tell his whole story with the sound off — from *hiding/imitating* to *fully himself.*

## 1. DANIEL — the hero (12)

- **Build & proportions:** wiry, all energy, slightly small for his age — a coiled spring. Animation proportions: larger head, big expressive eyes, long loose limbs that he doesn't fully control yet (he gestures with his whole body). Always *almost* in motion even when still — a tapping foot, a bouncing knee.
- **Face:** warm brown skin (Nigerian father / Spanish-Italian mother — a specific, in-between tone). Big, quick, curious eyes that dart (his attention is everywhere). An easy, slightly crooked grin. Eyebrows that do half his talking.
- **Hair:** dark, textured coils kept in a short-ish fro or twists — a little wild, never perfectly groomed (it moves when he moves; animators can let it carry rhythm).
- **Palette:** warm and saturated — terracotta, ochre, a pop of electric coral/orange. His color literally *spills past edges* in his happiest beats.
- **Shape language:** rounded, syncopated, irregular — no straight lines in him.
- **Silhouette:** the kid mid-gesture, limbs at odd joyful angles, hair haloing — unmistakably *too much for the frame*.
- **Wardrobe — the arc:**
  - *Start:* hand-me-downs that don't quite fit + (wrongly) a piece imitating TÌ's sleek look — he's trying to be someone else. Slightly buttoned-up, a little stiff.
  - *Finale:* his own thing — a layered street/praise fusion that's unmistakably him: comfortable, expressive, a touch of his mother's color and his father's pattern, room to move. The frame finally expands to hold him instead of him shrinking to fit.
- **Body language:** poly-rhythmic — hands, shoulders, feet all on slightly different beats. When he's truly himself, it resolves into the groove that pulls others in.
- **Personality in the design:** every visual choice says *more than the room can hold* — until the end, when that's the gift, not the problem.
- **CONCEPT-ART PROMPT:** *"Stylized 3D animated character, a 12-year-old mixed Nigerian/Spanish-Italian boy, warm brown skin, big expressive darting eyes, crooked grin, short wild textured dark coils, wiry small build mid-motion with loose long limbs, warm terracotta-and-coral layered street clothes with subtle cultural pattern, joyful too-much-energy pose, Pixar-warm but graphic lighting, faint musical motion-trails behind his hands, golden-hour glow, character turnaround sheet."*

## 2. STELLA — the co-lead (12)

- **Build & proportions:** contained, still, economical — the visual opposite of Daniel. Upright posture, hands often close to her body or precisely placed. Stillness that reads as *seeing*, not shyness.
- **Face:** Peruvian mother / Black American father — warm medium-brown skin, calm steady eyes that genuinely *look* (and sometimes look just past you, at the pattern behind you). A face that's quietly reading the room, not performing for it. When she's delighted by a pattern, a small private smile — earned, not given.
- **Hair:** dark, thick, worn in a practical, precise style — neat braids or a low controlled bun; nothing in her eyes (she needs to see).
- **Palette:** cool and exact — teal, slate, a clean accent (a single bright thread that means something). When her pattern-sight activates, fine luminous geometry blooms over the scene.
- **Shape language:** clean, tessellated, balanced — she's the only lead with deliberate straight lines, but they're *elegant*, not cold.
- **Silhouette:** still and upright, head slightly tilted as if listening to something no one else hears.
- **Wardrobe:** comfortable, sensory-smart (soft seams, layers she controls), with one self-chosen geometric motif she loves (architecture/rhythm patterns). Function and beauty together — never "quirky genius" costume.
- **Body language:** minimal, precise, intentional; stims rendered with respect as natural self-regulation, never a joke. When she dances, it's the most controlled, pattern-perfect movement on the floor.
- **Personality in the design:** she sees everything; the design makes *her* worth seeing — specific, warm, a full person, never "the autistic one."
- **CONCEPT-ART PROMPT:** *"Stylized 3D animated character, a calm 12-year-old Peruvian/Black American girl, warm medium-brown skin, steady observant eyes, neat dark braids, upright contained posture, cool teal-and-slate comfortable layered clothing with one elegant geometric pattern she loves, faint luminous tessellating gridlines blooming over the scene around her, dignified and specific, Pixar-warm graphic lighting, character turnaround sheet."*

## 3. NIA — the voice / love interest (12)

- **Build & proportions:** graceful, a little reserved; holds herself carefully, like she's keeping something in (she is — her voice). Opens up physically as her arc opens her voice.
- **Face:** expressive, soulful eyes that carry the grief she doesn't talk about; a warm face that's learned to stay quiet. The moment she finally sings, the whole face releases.
- **Hair:** soft, can move with music; a style that frames her face when she finally lifts her head to sing.
- **Palette:** twilight tones that warm — deep indigo/plum that bloom into gold the instant her voice returns (her color literally turns on with her sound).
- **Shape language:** rounded, gentle, with a held quality that loosens.
- **Silhouette:** at first, head slightly down, arms close — contained. At the Anthem: head up, throat open, one of the great "release" silhouettes of the film.
- **Wardrobe:** soft, modest, lovely; nothing flashy — she's not a prize, she's a person. A small keepsake from the loved one she lost (ties to why she stopped singing).
- **Body language:** still and protective, until the Anthem unlocks a fluid openness.
- **Personality in the design:** the design hides her gift, then reveals it — possibility returning after despair, made visible.
- **CONCEPT-ART PROMPT:** *"Stylized 3D animated character, a graceful reserved 12-year-old girl with soulful expressive eyes, soft framing hair, deep indigo-and-plum clothing warming to gold, a small keepsake necklace, posture protective then opening, mid-song head-up 'release' pose with throat open, Pixar-warm emotional lighting, character turnaround sheet."*

## 4. THEO — the steady anchor (13)

- **Build & proportions:** the biggest, most grounded kid in the crew — broad, solid, calm. Low center of gravity; he's the floor everyone stands on.
- **Face:** open, kind, a little tired around the eyes (he carries everyone). A warm, reassuring smile; the face you trust in a crisis.
- **Hair:** simple, neat, low-maintenance — he's not about himself.
- **Palette:** dependable earth tones — deep green, warm brown, a steady gold.
- **Shape language:** strong rounded rectangles — stability, reliability.
- **Silhouette:** broad, planted, often with a hand on someone else's shoulder.
- **Wardrobe:** practical, comfortable, captain-ish without trying — the one who carries the bag, brings the water.
- **Body language:** still, anchoring; the others orbit him. His arc-beat: the moment he finally *leans on someone else.*
- **Personality in the design:** looks like home base — until we learn even the rock needs holding.

## 5. LUCKY (Lucía Reyes-Nakamura) — the bridge (12)

- **Build & proportions:** quick, sharp, expressive — fast hands, fast wit; a live-wire who's funny on purpose.
- **Face:** Mexican mother / Japanese father — a specific blended look she's proud of; bright, mischievous eyes; an easy laugh that's never at anyone's expense.
- **Hair:** a bold, self-styled cut with a streak of color — she chooses her own look as her own answer to "which box am I in?"
- **Palette:** a confident clash that somehow works — she *is* the bridge, so two palettes meet in her (warm Mexican brights + cool Japanese ink-and-indigo).
- **Shape language:** angular but bouncy — sharp energy, warm heart.
- **Silhouette:** dynamic, hands talking, mid-quip.
- **Wardrobe:** intentional fusion — she wears both heritages at once, deliberately, joyfully.
- **Personality in the design:** her whole look refuses the either/or — proof you can be the seam, not the halfway.

## 6. TÌ (Tiago Malhi) — the rival (12)

- **Build & proportions:** the most physically *finished* kid in the film — controlled, athletic, a trained fighter's poise. Where Daniel is loose and spilling, TÌ is contained and exact. Stands like he's already won.
- **Face:** strikingly handsome, composed, Brazilian; a practiced, camera-ready confidence — and, in private flickers, something hollow behind the eyes. The audience should *like* him on sight (essential).
- **Hair:** immaculate, sculpted, not a strand wrong — the opposite of Daniel's wild coils.
- **Palette:** gold and obsidian, mirror-polish; beautiful and airless. As the Fracture's influence shows, faint grey-violet wrongness edges his world.
- **Shape language:** sharp, symmetrical, perfect — the design embodiment of "dominance mistaken for confidence."
- **Silhouette:** flawless, vertical, alone on a pedestal — he never stands in a circle.
- **Wardrobe:** couture-level performance wear, dynasty crest, sponsor sheen — luxury that looks like a cage. In the sacrifice, it's the perfect surface that cracks.
- **Body language:** total control — until the finale, when his body *commits before his mind* (he steps in front of the Fracture), then fails him; his face does all the work as he can't get up.
- **Personality in the design:** everything about him is impressive and nothing about him is *held* — the mirror of who Daniel could become.
- **CONCEPT-ART PROMPT:** *"Stylized 3D animated character, a strikingly handsome composed 12-year-old Brazilian boy, immaculate sculpted dark hair, athletic trained-fighter poise, gold-and-obsidian couture dance costume with a dynasty crest and faint sponsor sheen, flawless symmetrical design, confident camera-ready expression with a hint of hollowness in the eyes, cold mirror-polish lighting with subtle grey-violet edge, character turnaround sheet."*

## 7. JULIAN HARTLEY — the Grand Inquisitor / guide

- **Build & proportions:** tall, lean, impeccable, still — power that never needs volume. Stillness is his weapon.
- **Face:** British, sharp, weathered-handsome, late 50s; eyes that wait for you to find your own mistake. Two completely different faces: the public mask (granite) and the private father (soft — the Ratatouille moment).
- **Hair:** silver, precise.
- **Palette:** charcoal, slate, a single warm tell (a wedding band, a scarf his daughter picked) that hints at the private man.
- **Shape language:** vertical, controlled, elegant lines.
- **Silhouette:** the seated figure who stays seated while everyone else stands — then rises last.
- **Wardrobe:** flawless tailoring, theatrical-judge gravitas; backstage, one button undone, sleeves up, holding his daughter — visually a different man.
- **Personality in the design:** the look says "feared"; the private warmth says "only human" — and the gap is the whole point.

## 8. MAMÁ ÀBÈNÍ — the matriarch

- **Build & proportions:** tiny, ancient, impossibly old — but with a spine of iron and hands that still move when she hums. Not frail-pathetic; *formidable.*
- **Face:** deep-lined, luminous, Yoruba; eyes that have seen everything and still find Daniel delightful. The warmest face in the film.
- **Hair:** silver, wrapped in a beautiful gele (head wrap) with meaning in its cloth.
- **Palette:** the warmest in the whole movie — golds, deep indigos, sunset oranges; she's lit like home.
- **Shape language:** small rounded forms, soft but unbreakable.
- **Silhouette:** the small wrapped figure at the kitchen table, humming, that you'd know anywhere — and, after she's gone, the empty chair the family keeps setting.
- **Wardrobe:** rich traditional Yoruba cloth (consultant-guided), worn every day like armor of belonging; one piece passes to Daniel.
- **Personality in the design:** she looks like the one place Daniel was always seen — so losing her is the floor dropping, and her hum becoming the world's song is the payoff.
- **CONCEPT-ART PROMPT:** *"Stylized 3D animated character, a tiny impossibly-old Yoruba great-grandmother with a deeply lined luminous warm face and knowing delighted eyes, silver hair in a beautiful patterned gele head wrap, rich traditional indigo-and-gold cloth, seated at a warm kitchen table mid-hum with expressive hands, the warmest golden home lighting in the film, formidable not frail, character turnaround sheet."*

## 9. COACH WREN — the eye that quit the rubric (50s)

- **Build & proportions:** wiry, watchful, economical; a former dancer's carriage with a slight old injury she ignores. She observes more than she moves.
- **Face:** sharp, perceptive, a little worn; the face of someone who paid a price for knowing the truth. Rare warmth that means everything when it lands (on Daniel).
- **Hair:** practical, greying, pulled back.
- **Palette:** muted, lived-in — worn denim, faded competition jacket she's earned and outgrown.
- **Silhouette:** arms crossed, watching from the edge, seeing what the judges can't.
- **Personality in the design:** looks like someone the system used up — which is exactly why her recognizing Daniel is the system being overruled by the eye that sees.

---

## 10. ÌJÀLÁ — the prophet (seen only in traces)

- **Approach:** we never meet him directly — he appears in *evidence*: a weathered journal, a carving, a fragment of song. Design him through artifacts and a few stylized, sepia/ochre flashback glimpses — ancient, intense, misunderstood, mid-chant. Yoruba hunter-poet aesthetic (consultant-guided), staff and beads, eyes fixed on a future no one else believes in.
- **Palette:** aged ochre, bronze, deep shadow — the look of memory and prophecy.
- **Personality in the design:** the man everyone called crazy who turned out to be right — drawn with reverence, never spectacle.

## 11. THE FIVE HOUSE LEADS (design shorthand)

- **Kwame (House of the Drum):** grounded, powerful build; earth/indigo/brass palette; carries the talking drum like a part of his body; cloth that means lineage; silhouette = planted, drum at hip.
- **Marisol (House of Carnival):** vivid, kinetic, joyful; riot-of-color palette, carnival textures, feathers/street-paint; silhouette = mid-spin, arms wide; capoeira spring in her stance.
- **Min-jun (House of One Body):** precise, monochrome with one accent; severe perfect lines; silhouette = part of a flawless block formation, until his arc lets him break the line into something human.
- **Esmé (House of the Braid):** many textures, no single palette; flamenco posture + North African + Balkan + club fusion; silhouette = one figure wearing four traditions at once; carries the film's "grace" warmth.

---

## 12. THE FRACTURE — the antagonist force (not a character — a presence)

- **Visual:** stylized shadow-silhouettes; **the absence of rhythm made visible.** Patterns break, geometry stutters, color drains to grey-violet, dancers desync. Never a monster face, never gore — *wrongness*, not horror. A 7-year-old should feel unsettled, never have nightmares.
- **Rule:** you know it's near because the *music goes slightly wrong and people stop moving together.* That's the whole language — keep it readable to kids.

## HOW TO USE THIS (for an artist or an AI image tool)

1. **Start with the visual system (§0)** — lock the rendering style, color-coding, and shape language *first*, so all characters feel like one film.
2. **Design the core five together** (Daniel, Stella, Nia, TÌ, Mamá Àbèní) so they read as a family of shapes and a balanced palette before adding the rest.
3. **Use the concept-art prompts** as starting points for image generation, then iterate — they're written to be pasted into a text-to-image tool.
4. **Two consistency rules:** every character must read in **silhouette alone**, and **Daniel's start vs. finale costume must tell his arc with the sound off.**
5. **Cultural designs (Mamá Àbèní's cloth, ÌJÀLÁ, the Houses) need consultant review** before they're locked — the bible flags where.
