## 2A — Global Style and Format

**Animation Style:** 3D CGI in the tradition of Pixar and Illumination — characters have soft, round forms, expressive oversized eyes, and smooth motion with slight cartoon exaggeration. Think *Bluey* 's warmth in a 3D world. Every element is safe, bright, and child-friendly.

**Render Feel:** No visible ink outlines. Form, color, and soft directional light define character edges. Shadows exist but are always soft, warm-toned, and never cast over a character's face in a frightening way.

**Primary Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1280×720 generation via Higgsfield; masters at 1920×1080 target). YouTube Shorts (9:16) variants for selected musical clips.

| Golden Brown | #C47A35 | Skin tone anchor (Sunny's palette) |
| Leo Red | #CC2200 | Leo's signature color, energy accents |

**Saturation Level:** Mid-to-vivid. Reads clean and bright on consumer screens and children's tablets at all times. No desaturated, gray-dominant, or low-contrast scenes ever.

**Lighting Mood (default):** Warm afternoon daylight. Soft directional sun from camera-right. Ambient fill from all sides. Zero dark areas in any frame a child will see.

### Prohibited Visual Elements
- No hard drop shadows or stark shadow contrast
- No photorealistic textures (no real photo grass, sky, bark, etc.)
- No desaturated or gray-dominant scenes
- No threatening compositions (looming objects, extreme low-angle characters, dramatic underlighting)
- No adult-coded visual references of any kind
- No unnamed/undescribed background characters — all BG figures are stylized rounded forms in show palette

### Title / Logo Treatment
"Sunny and the Crew!" appears in rounded bubble letters. "Sunny" is yellow (#FFCC00) with a warm sun-glow effect behind it. "and the Crew!" is white with a soft coral outline (#FF7F5C). Letters bounce in one at a time on each downbeat. The exclamation mark pops in last with a star-burst. Logo holds 2 full seconds on final frame of every intro sequence. Appears again on last frame of every episode outro.

## 2B — Core Environments

### World Geography
All locations sit within a three-block radius in Marigold Grove — a cheerful, diverse American neighborhood. Sunny's house is on the main street. The park is a three-minute walk. The classroom is implied to be one block over. All travel is on foot or bicycle. No car travel appears in the show's visual grammar.

---

### LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET — Sunny's Street

**Visual Description:** Wide, sun-drenched suburban street flanked by colorful houses — yellow, mint green, warm coral, sky blue — each with a tidy yard and a distinct personality. Clean sidewalks occasionally feature chalked hopscotch squares. Manicured green lawns give way to leafy trees. Blue sky, 2–3 large fluffy white clouds. Late-afternoon light warms the scene from camera-right. Background elements: a red fire hydrant, a pastel mailbox, a parked bicycle leaned against a fence, birds on a wire.

**Emotional Purpose:** Openness, invitation, freedom to run. The whole world is here and it's exactly the right size.

**Key Interactive Props:** Sidewalk chalk, a jump rope coiled on a stoop, a red wagon parked by a driveway.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** Sunny runs toward camera and past it — silhouette fills frame — cut on contact. Or: camera pans left along the street to reveal the destination.

---

### LOC_SUNNY_PORCH — Sunny's Front Porch

**Visual Description:** Warm wooden porch with painted floorboards (yellow-cream). A bright yellow front door with a small round window at child-eye height. Two potted marigold plants flank the three steps. A welcome mat reads "HELLO!" in cheerful block letters. The porch railing has unlit string lights — ambient decor only. Steps lead to a short flagstone path, then the green lawn.

**Emotional Purpose:** Intimacy and belonging. This is where Sunny thinks, rests, and says goodbye. Safe-home energy.

**Key Interactive Props:** A small wooden chair sized for Sunny, a chalkboard sign on the railing, a potted plant she tends.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** Characters walk up the path and sit on steps. Goodbye scenes end with Sunny on the steps, waving. Camera pulls back as she waves.

---

### LOC_BACKYARD — Sunny's Backyard

**Visual Description:** Enclosed backyard with a white-painted wood fence. Green grass with a bare-earth garden patch: sunflowers, tomatoes, and marigolds in neat rows. A round wooden picnic table with two benches sits center-left. A bird feeder on a pole stands center-right. An easel with a blank pad lives beside the table — always available. Neighbor rooftops and treetops just visible above the fence. Light comes over the fence at a warm angle.

**Emotional Purpose:** Collaborative and playful — working-together energy. Problem-solving, activities, and teaching moments happen here.

**Key Interactive Props:** Gardening trowel, bright yellow watering can, magnifying glass on the table, a basket for collecting things, the easel with letter/concept cards.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** The wooden fence gate swings open — characters walk through. Exit: gate swings shut as camera pulls back to a wide.

---

### LOC_CLASSROOM — Sunny's Classroom

**Visual Description:** Bright preschool classroom bathed in natural light from large rounded windows. Small wooden desks in a loose circle. Alphabet posters line every wall — each letter paired with a 3D-style cartoon illustration. A bookshelf holds thick picture books with visible colorful spines. A large easel with alphabet cards stands at the room's front. A big round rainbow rug defines the gathering area. Star stickers dot the ceiling. A large red apple always sits on the front table.

**Emotional Purpose:** Learning feels safe and celebrated here. Curiosity welcome.

**Key Interactive Props:** Alphabet blocks, the Letter of the Day card on the easel, a tray of crayons, the big red apple, a large magnifying glass.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** Camera pushes in through the door's round window → door swings open. Exit: camera pulls back through the door as characters wave.

---

### LOC_PARK — The Neighborhood Park

**Visual Description:** A cheerful community park with a primary-color plastic playground (slide, two swings, a climbing dome). Green grass with clover patches. Two large oak trees provide dappled shade on one side. A paved path curves through the space. Blue sky with cotton-ball clouds. Stylized background children (rounded forms, diverse, no detailed faces) play in the distance.

**Emotional Purpose:** High energy, social, movement-forward. Chorus peaks and celebration moments.

**Key Interactive Props:** Swing set, sandbox with plastic tools, a low balance beam, a rubber ball.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** Characters run in through a low gate. Exit: camera wide — characters wave and run back toward gate.

---

### LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS — Camera Direct Address Zone

**Visual Description:** Sunny faces camera against a solid warm background — either Sunny Yellow (#FFCC00) or a soft sky-blue gradient (#B8E8F9 → #FFFFFF). No props. No background characters. This zone exists only in the camera's mind — it's where Sunny "breaks the wall" to speak directly to the child at home.

**Emotional Purpose:** Pure connection. Just Sunny and the viewer.

**Key Interactive Props:** None — her expressions and gestures ARE the prop.

**Arrival/Departure Cue:** Sunny turns her body to face camera with a gentle lean-in. Cut back to location with Sunny turning back into the scene.

## 2D — Visual Motifs and Transition System

### Recurring Visual Motifs

**1. Sunny's Sun Burst**
A stylized burst of yellow rays erupts from behind Sunny when she makes a discovery or has a big idea. Six to eight straight rays in flat 2D style over the 3D scene. Color: bright #FFCC00. Duration: 8 frames. Appears on every major "aha" moment.

**2. Letter of the Day Badge**
A rounded rectangle badge with the episode's featured letter pops into frame in chalk-style block font. Badge is cream (#FFF8ED) with a coral border (#FF7F5C). The letter rotates once (clockwise) then settles. Used exclusively on the first letter reveal of each episode.

**3. Musical Note Trail**
During songs, floating musical notes (quarter notes and eighth notes) drift upward from characters who are singing. Notes use the show palette, soft and translucent. Speed matches the track BPM.

**4. Wonder Dots**
When a character is puzzling something out, 3–5 soft dots appear near their head — muted yellow and blue, bobbing gently. Replaced by a bright lightbulb pop (#FFCC00) the moment the answer arrives.

**5. Bounce Underscore**
On every chorus hit and major beat accent, the entire scene performs a subtle 2-frame scale bounce (105% → 100%). The effect is felt rather than consciously noticed — it keeps the visuals alive with the music.

**6. Chalk Word**
When a new concept word is introduced, it appears in chalk-style text floating near the relevant object. Font: rounded, slightly wobbly, as if a child drew it. Color matches the Letter of the Day badge border.

**7. Heart Pop**
A small red heart pops from near a character's chest when they feel proud, loved, or celebrated. Used once per episode maximum — never overused.

**8. Sparkle Trail**
When the crew is called or assembled, a soft sparkle trail (cyan #7EC8E3) traces a curved path connecting the characters in frame. Plays once, 12 frames.

---

### Transition System

**TYPE A — The Bounce Wipe**
A character jumps or runs toward camera. Their silhouette fills the frame. Cut mid-jump — new location is revealed as they "land." Used for high-energy scene changes.

**TYPE B — The Warm Fade**
A golden glow from frame edges fades scene to a warm flat color (yellow or coral), then fades into the new location. Used for emotional or quieter transitions.

**TYPE C — The Star Burst**
A flash of white light from center frame, expanding star-shaped, fills frame and then contracts to reveal the new scene. Used for song launches and major "aha" moments.

### Beat-Sync System

| Musical Event | Visual Response |
|---|---|
| Downbeat (kick) | 2-frame scale bounce on all foreground characters |
| Chorus drop | Star Burst transition OR fast cut to hero wide shot |
| Hand clap in track | Characters clap in sync; cut lands on the clap impact frame |
| Lyric emphasis word | Chalk Word floats near the relevant object |
| Bridge arrival | Camera pulls back to reveal wider scene or new character |
| Outro sting | Show logo animates in; characters hold final pose |
| Call-and-response pause | Viewer Interaction beat; character turns to camera, leans in |
