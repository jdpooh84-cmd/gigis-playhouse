# EP01 — Movement, Games & Real-Kid Interaction Plan
Design plan for making EP01 feel like real 3–7-year-olds playing, moving, and interacting — at the intro-song engagement level. Inclusive of kids on/off the spectrum, cognitive differences, and ASL / visual-cue users. **Plan only — no rebuild until confirmed.** 2026-07-14.

---

## SECTION 1 — Movement & Game Worksheet (ages 3–7, all neurotypes & abilities)

### A. Whole-body / gross-motor (the "get them off the couch" moves)
- **Syllable jumps / stomps** — one jump or stomp per name syllable (KO-DA = 2 stomps). Big, mirror-framed, real kid timing.
- **Grow big / shrink small** — curl tiny → stretch tall into an "A-pose." Doubles as a feelings scale (see F).
- **Airplane arms / rocket arms** — arms out, lean and glide (ties directly to the song's airplane verse).
- **March & freeze** — march in place, freeze on a clear signal. Freeze games build impulse control and are a spectrum favorite because the rule is simple and predictable.
- **Animal moves** — hop like a frog, stomp like a bear (quick, silly, resets attention).

### B. Fine-motor / tracing
- **Sky-writing** — trace a letter big in the air with one finger (whole-arm = easier for little hands than pencil).
- **Palm-tracing** — trace the letter on your own palm (self-regulating, quiet option for kids who need less stimulation).
- **Rainbow trace** — trace the same letter 3 times "in 3 colors" (repetition without boredom).

### C. Rhythm / clapping patterns
- **Name claps** — clap once per syllable of your name (the episode's signature game).
- **Clap-back / echo** — leader claps a short pattern, everyone claps it back (call-and-response; core to music engagement).
- **Body percussion** — clap–pat–stomp combos (multi-sensory; good for regulation).
- **Partner pat-a-cake** — two kids clap together (turn-taking + connection).

### D. Turn-taking & social / peer-interaction
- **Name circle** — each kid steps in, says their name + a move; everyone **copies** it. (turn-taking + mirroring + being seen)
- **Copy-me / mirror game** — one leads a move, others follow. Non-verbal kids can lead with body only.
- **Cheer each other on** — when a friend tries something hard, the crew cheers by name ("You got it, Bram!").
- **Pass the clap / pass the move** — hand a clap around the circle (waiting for your turn, sustained attention).

### E. Thinking / guessing games
- **Guess the first letter** — hear the sound "/mmm/", guess the letter *and* whose name it starts ("M — Mia!"). Sound + shape + name together.
- **I-spy an A** — spot an "A-word" thing (apple, airplane) — ties to the song.
- **Which one is mine?** — match a kid to their starting letter.

### F. Feelings / regulation (co-regulation, spectrum- & AAC-friendly)
- **Feelings postures** — show the feeling with the whole body: *small/shy* = curl up, *proud* = tall A-pose, *excited* = wiggle. Feelings become **visible**, so a non-verbal or deaf child can "say" them without words.
- **How big is your feeling?** — body scale from tiny to huge.
- **Calm move** — "smell the flower, blow the bubble" breathing; a reset any kid can copy.

### G. Audience-join / parasocial (kids at home play too)
- **Direct invite + WAIT** — "Your turn — clap your name!" then a **real 3-second pause** with an expectant look. Wait-time is non-negotiable for kids who process slower or use AAC.
- **Screen high-five / screen tap** — "High-five the screen!" gives a physical beat of connection.
- **Did-you-do-it callback** — "I saw that! Amazing!" validates the at-home child.

### Inclusive design rules (baked into every game above)
1. **Multi-modal always** — every instruction is *said, shown on the mouth, shown with the body, and shown with a letter/icon overlay.* Deaf/ASL and pre-verbal kids never depend on audio.
2. **Predictable & repeatable** — same game, same cue every time; a consistent "ready signal" (Sunny's hands-up "1–2–3").
3. **Clear start/stop, no sensory surprises** — no sudden loud stings; calm transitions.
4. **Wait-time (3s)** — after every audience prompt, a real pause. Processing time = access.
5. **No-fail + choice** — "clap OR tap," "trace it your way." A low-motor option for physical differences; nobody can do it "wrong."
6. **One idea at a time** — short concrete sentences; one action per beat.
7. **Model → invite → do-together → celebrate** (I do / we do / you do).
8. **Co-regulation** — name the feeling, show it in the body, offer a calming move.
9. **Peer warmth** — kids cheer each other by name; mistakes are met with encouragement, not correction.

## SECTION 2 — Scene-by-scene action/game remap (SEC-02 / 03 / 05)

### SEC-02 — Story Setup (0:44–2:08) · "Name Day"
**Now:** kids stand in a line, say their name + clap; Pipa says "my name's not special" (still, sad).
**Remap → the NAME CIRCLE GAME + BIG/SMALL FEELINGS:**
- Each kid **steps into the circle**, says their name **while doing a syllable-move**, and the others **copy it**: Koda **stomps** "KO-DA," Mimi **bounces** "MI-MI," Mia **spins** "MI-A," Leo claps + "rocket arms" "LE-O." (turn-taking + copy-me + gross motor)
- Kids talk **to each other**: "Whoa, do it again!" / "My turn!" / high-fives — not reciting to camera.
- **Pipa's beat becomes the "Big & Small Feelings" game** (see Game 2): Pipa curls **small**; Sunny kneels and invites her — and the audience — to "show me small… now show me BIG and proud," everyone growing into an A-pose. SEL is now *physical*, not a talking-head.
- **Audience:** "Your turn — clap YOUR name!" + 3s wait.

### SEC-03 — Practice / Game + song lead-in (2:08–4:02)
**Now:** trace an A, name-claps, Bram's letter wobbles, "can we sing the song?"
**Remap → GUESS-THE-LETTER + SKY-WRITE RELAY + CHEER-HIM-ON:**
- **Guess the First Letter game** (Game 3): Sunny makes the sound "/mmm/…", crew + audience guess "M — Mia!" (sound→letter→name; 3s wait). Runs through K, M, L, B, P, S.
- **Sky-write relay:** each kid traces their letter in the air; a partner **copies** it. Correct glyph overlaid (the literacy fix). Fine-motor + copy-me.
- **Bram's retry = resilience beat:** his "B" goes wobbly ("uh oh — oops!"); the crew **cheers him on by name** ("You can do it, Bram!"); he tries again and lands it; everyone jumps and celebrates. Models mistakes-are-okay + peer support.
- **Jumping name-claps:** kids **jump** each syllable; audience jumps too (attention re-set right before the song).
- **Song lead-in (the fix):** Leo — "Can we sing the A song?" → Sunny to camera — **"Let's sing the A song! Sing with us!"** → SEC-04.

### SEC-04 — Song (4:02–5:47) · now PERFORMED
- Crew **visibly sings** (Sunny leads verses, everyone sings the chorus hook — the lip-sync fix). Movement cue every chorus (clap on "A-A-A," airplane arms on the airplane verse). **Audience:** "Sing it with us! Jump on A!"

### SEC-05 — Reflection / Outro (5:47–6:42)
**Now:** recap lines, praise, take-home mission (mostly talking).
**Remap → COPY-THE-MOVES RECAP + PROUD POSE + MISSION-AS-GAME:**
- **"Let's do our moves one more time!"** — fast copy-the-movement recap: name-clap → A-trace → big-proud pose, kids + audience together (active recap, not a montage of talking).
- **Proud check:** Pipa hits her big proud A-pose; "Show me YOUR proud!" (audience, 3s wait).
- **Take-home mission as a game:** "At home today: clap your name, make an A, and show me PROUD!" — three concrete do-able actions, each shown.
- Catchphrase + goodbye wave to camera.

**Where the games land vs. attention dips:** a game at the SEC-02→03 seam, the guess-the-letter game mid-SEC-03 (the classic mid-episode dip), the jump-claps right before the song, and the copy-recap opening SEC-05 (post-song re-engage).

## SECTION 3 — Sample rewritten dialogue (kids talking WHILE they play)

Real kid cadence, short lines, spoken *during* the action, ElevenLabs voices, with audience invites + 3s waits. Action in **(bold)**.

### Scene A — "The Name-Clap Game" (SEC-03)
- **Sunny** *(bouncing on toes)*: "Okay okay — me first! Watch this!"
- **Sunny** *(2 claps + stomp)*: "SUN-NY!"
- **Leo** *(jumping in)*: "Me next me next!" *(claps twice, spins)* "LE-O!"
- **Mia** *(giggling)*: "Mine's a bouncy one — ready?" *(stomps)* "MI-A!"
- **Koda** *(to Mia, copying)*: "Ooh do it again!" — **Mia + Koda together**: "MI-A!"
- **Bram** *(tracing in the air, wobbles)*: "Mine goes… uh-oh — oops!"
- **Mimi** *(cheering, hands up)*: "Try again, Bram! You got it!"
- **Bram** *(big confident trace)*: "B — BRAM! I did it!"
- **Crew** *(jumping, cheering)*: "Yaaay Bram!"
- **Sunny** *(to camera, expectant)*: "Your turn! What's YOUR name? Clap it with me!" — ***(3-second wait, everyone looks at the audience)***
- **Sunny** *(hand to the screen)*: "I heard you! High-five the screen!"

### Scene B — "Big & Small Feelings" (SEC-02, Pipa's beat)
- **Pipa** *(shrinking, quiet)*: "My name's just… little." *(curls up small)*
- **Sunny** *(kneeling close, gentle)*: "Can you show me little?" — *(Pipa makes herself tiny)*
- **Sunny**: "I see it. Now… can you show me BIG?" *(Sunny + crew grow tall into A-pose)*
- **Leo** *(stretching up)*: "Big like a rocket!"
- **Mimi** *(on tiptoes)*: "Big big BIG!"
- **Pipa** *(slowly rising, tiny smile)*: "…big?"
- **Crew** *(cheering)*: "Big and proud, Pipa!"
- **Pipa** *(arms up, proud A-pose)*: "PI-PA!"
- **Sunny** *(to camera)*: "Show me YOUR big and proud!" — ***(3-second wait)*** — "There it is! Amazing."

Both scenes: kids talk **to each other**, play **while** talking (clap/stomp/spin/trace/grow), take turns, copy each other, cheer by name, and pull the audience in with a real pause.

## Two recurring audience games (on-screen kids AND kids at home)
- **GAME 1 — "Clap Your Name!"** — syllable name-claps. Taught in SEC-02, played in SEC-03, sung in the song, recapped in SEC-05. Consistent cue + 3s wait. Kids at home clap their own name.
- **GAME 2 — "Show Me Your Feeling!"** — body-scale feelings (small ↔ big/proud). At Pipa's sad beat (SEC-02), her turnaround (SEC-03/song), and the reflection (SEC-05). Word-free = works for pre-verbal, ASL, and spectrum kids.
- *(Optional)* **GAME 3 — "Guess the First Letter"** — sound→letter→name, mid-SEC-03.
