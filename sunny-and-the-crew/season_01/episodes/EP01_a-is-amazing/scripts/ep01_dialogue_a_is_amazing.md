**Runtime:** ~5:49 total
**Age Target:** 2–5

---

### WORD COUNT RULES
- Lead characters (Sunny, Leo, Mia, Nana Blossom): max 8 words per spoken line
- Mimi: max 6 words per spoken line
- Viewer pauses: 3-second hold (noted inline)
- Apple: minimum 3 appearances
- Airplane: minimum 3 appearances
- Letter "A": minimum 5 appearances
- No negative language; no "you're wrong," no shame
- 1 warm mistake-and-correction beat
- Explicit emotional closure before goodbye

## PART 2 — STORY SETUP (8 BEATS)
**Runtime:** 0:44–2:25
**Runtime per beat noted below**

---

### BEAT 1 — SUNNY DIRECT ADDRESS (0:44–1:00)
**Location:** LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS
**Characters:** Sunny (solo)

SUNNY:
*(big wave, bright smile, facing camera)*
Hey friends — it's me, Sunny!

*(bounces once, leans in)*
I'm SO glad you're here today.

*(holds up one finger)*
We're going to learn something amazing.

*(pause — gestures to viewer)*
You ready? Let's go!

---

### BEAT 2 — APPLE DISCOVERY (1:00–1:15)
**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH
**Characters:** Sunny, Mia, Nana Blossom

*(Sunny and Mia arrive at Nana Blossom's porch. Nana Blossom holds a basket of apples.)*

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(warm, wide arms)*
Oh! You two came just in time.

MIA:
*(eyes wide, pointing)*
Nana Blossom — what is that?

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(lifts a shiny red apple)*
This? It's an apple, sweet Mia.

MIA:
*(gasping)*
An apple! It's so red and round!

SUNNY:
*(turning to camera)*
Friends — did you see that apple?

*(holds up three fingers, counts)*
Apple! Apple! Apple!

---

### BEAT 3 — AIRPLANE DISCOVERY (1:15–1:30)
**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET
**Characters:** Sunny, Leo, Mia

*(A paper airplane sails into frame. Leo runs in after it, laughing.)*

LEO:
*(running, arms wide like wings)*
It's flying! My airplane is flying!

MIA:
*(clapping)*
Leo! That airplane went so far!

LEO:
*(catching the airplane, holds it up)*
I made this airplane all by myself!

SUNNY:
*(turning to camera)*
Friends — did you see that airplane?

*(swoops arm like a plane)*
Airplane! Whoooosh!

---

### BEAT 4 — WARM MISTAKE AND CORRECTION (1:30–1:45)
**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH
**Characters:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Nana Blossom

*(Nana Blossom holds the apple. Leo holds his airplane. Both are showing off their things.)*

SUNNY:
*(thinking, finger to chin)*
Hmm. An apple and an airplane.

*(to friends)*
What do they have in common?

LEO:
*(excited, confident)*
Oh! I know! They're both red!

*(pause — Mia looks at the airplane, which is white)*

MIA:
*(gently, pointing)*
Leo — your airplane is white, though.

LEO:
*(looks at it, laughs softly)*
Oh! Ha. Yeah. You're right, Mia.

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(kind smile, not correcting, just wondering)*
Hmm. I wonder what they DO share.

SUNNY:
*(to camera — viewer pause)*
Friends, can YOU think of something?

*(3-SECOND HOLD — viewer response pause)*

---

### BEAT 5 — NANA BLOSSOM REVEALS LETTER A (1:45–2:00)
**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH
**Characters:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Nana Blossom

*(Nana Blossom reaches behind her and produces a big card with a bright red letter A.)*

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(holding up the A card)*
Apple — and airplane — both start with A!

SUNNY:
*(eyes go wide)*
A! The letter A!

LEO:
*(snapping fingers)*
That's it! A for apple!

MIA:
*(clapping, spinning)*
And A for airplane! Yes, yes, YES!

SUNNY:
*(to camera)*
A is the very FIRST letter — EVER!

*(holds up A card toward camera)*
Look — the letter A!

---

### BEAT 6 — CALL AND RESPONSE (2:00–2:15)
**Location:** LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS
**Characters:** Sunny (facing camera)

SUNNY:
*(leaning forward, cup hands around mouth)*
Okay friends — say it with me!

*(points to camera)*
What letter does APPLE start with?

*(3-SECOND HOLD — viewer response pause)*

*(big nod)*
A! That's RIGHT — the letter A!

*(sweeps arm like plane)*
And what letter does AIRPLANE start with?

*(points and waits — beat)*
A again! A is AMAZING, friends!

---

### BEAT 7 — MIMI SUGGESTS SINGING (2:15–2:25)
**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET
**Characters:** Sunny, Mia, Leo, Mimi

*(Mimi runs in barefoot, skidding to a stop, grinning ear to ear.)*

MIMI:
*(tugging Sunny's sleeve)*
Sunny! Can we SING about it?

SUNNY:
*(laughing, to crew)*
You heard Mimi — song time!

MIA:
*(grabbing Leo's hand, jumping)*
Let's sing! Let's sing! Let's SING!

LEO:
*(holding airplane up like a conductor's baton)*
One — two — A — GO!

---

### BEAT 8 — SONG COUNT-IN AND STAR BURST (2:25–2:44)
**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET → LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS
**Characters:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Mimi

*(The whole crew huddles together, bouncing in place.)*

SUNNY:
*(to camera, biggest smile)*
Okay friends — sing with us!

ALL CREW:
*(together — big jump, arms out)*
ONE — TWO — A — LET'S GO!

*(STAR BURST TRANSITION — music begins)*

## PART 4 — WRAP-UP DIALOGUE
**Runtime:** 5:04–5:36
**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH
**Characters:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Nana Blossom

*(Song ends. Crew is breathing happy and glowy. Nana Blossom puts her arms around them.)*

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(squeezing them close)*
You all did such a good job today.

SUNNY:
*(to camera)*
We learned SO much today, friends.

*(holds up apple)*
We found an apple — starts with A!

LEO:
*(swoops his airplane)*
And an airplane — starts with A too!

MIA:
*(making A shape with two fingers)*
And the letter A — is amazing!

SUNNY:
*(voice warm, locked on camera)*
I am SO proud of you today.

*(leans in)*
You sang. You listened. You LEARNED.

*(pause — soft, sincere)*
That makes ME really happy, friends.

NANA BLOSSOM:
*(to the kids and camera)*
Before you go — one A challenge.

*(taps chin, thinking face)*
Find one A word at your home.

SUNNY:
*(excited)*
Apple? Airplane? Ant? Arm? Art?

*(laughing)*
So many A words — go find one!

LEO:
*(to camera, waving his airplane)*
Tell us what A word you found!
