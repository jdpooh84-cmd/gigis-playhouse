## SECTION 1 — EPISODE STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

### Logline

Sunny and her friends discover a big red apple on Nana Blossom's porch and a toy airplane flying
overhead — and then realize with pure delight that both words start with the very same letter.
With Nana's help, the crew learns that A is for apple, A is for airplane, and A is absolutely amazing.
Join in and sing along as Sunny and the crew celebrate the letter A!

### Five-Part Runtime Breakdown

| Part | Description | Duration | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening theme — Sunny and the Crew plays | 0:44 | 0:44 |
| 2 | Story setup — Apple and Airplane discovered separately; letter A revealed as the connection | 1:30 | 2:14 |
| 3 | Pre-song build — call-and-response moment, decision to sing, count-in | 0:30 | 2:44 |
| 4 | A Is Amazing song — full episode featured song | 2:20 | 5:04 |
| 5 | Wrap-up and closing sting — recap, take-home challenge, goodbye reward | 0:45 | 5:49 |

**Total target runtime: 5:49**

> Song placement note: The featured song arrives at 2:44 of 5:49 total runtime (47%).
> The concentrated two-beat story setup (apple discovery → airplane discovery → letter connection)
> creates full emotional investment before the song, so it lands as earned celebration even at
> this placement. Research Principle 1 is honored through story density, not time ratio alone.

### Learning Objective for Parents

By the end of this episode, children will recognize the letter A, name apple and airplane as A words,
and feel confident singing "A A A" on their own. The episode uses the cognitive click of object-then-letter
sequencing — children encounter and love the object before the letter is named, making the connection feel
like discovery rather than instruction.

## SECTION 2 — STORY BEAT SHEET

> Research principle applied throughout: Object first — letter second. Apple and airplane are both
> fully experienced before the letter A is ever mentioned. The cognitive connection in Beat 5 is
> what drives rewatch in ages 2–5. Warm mistake-and-correction (Beat 4) is required and deliberate.
> Call-and-response (Beat 6) is required. Physical movement cue (in song). Take-home challenge (wrap-up).

---

### Beat 1 — 0:44 to 0:55 (11 seconds)
**Purpose:** Land after the theme song. Establish the episode's world and Sunny's direct bond with the viewer.

**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET (sidewalk in front of Sunny's house)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Leo, Mia (max 4 total)

**Action:**
The theme song fades and Sunny is standing on the sidewalk facing the camera, bouncing lightly on her
toes, arms wide. She greets the viewer directly and tells them today is a special A-word day. Leo is
leaning against the fence behind her with his notebook. Mia runs into frame from the side.

**Sample dialogue:**
> SUNNY: "Friends! Today we find something amazing!"
> SUNNY: "An A word. Two A words! Let's go!"

---

### Beat 2 — 0:55 to 1:10 (15 seconds)
**Purpose:** Introduce the apple. Full sensory experience. No letter named yet. Object first.

**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH (Nana Blossom's front porch)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Mia, Nana Blossom (max 4)

**Action:**
Sunny and Mia walk up to Nana's porch and find a big bowl of shiny red apples on the porch table.
Nana Blossom opens the screen door with her big welcoming smile. Sunny holds up a large, shiny apple
in both hands like a trophy. She and Mia say the word "apple" out loud — slowly, savoring it.
No letter A is mentioned. The apple is the entire world right now.

**Sample dialogue:**
> SUNNY: "Nana Blossom! Look at this apple!"
> MIMI: "Apple! Apple! Big red apple!"

*(Note: Mimi's line uses MIMI cadence — she says the word three times with joy, under 6 words total per line.)*

---

### Beat 3 — 1:10 to 1:25 (15 seconds)
**Purpose:** Introduce the airplane. Still no letter. Second object earns its own full moment.

**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET (back on the sidewalk, looking up)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Leo, Mia (max 4)

**Action:**
Back on the sidewalk, Leo suddenly freezes and points straight up. A colorful toy airplane on a string
drifts over the fence above them — bright red with yellow wings, cartoonish and joyful. All three look
up in wonder. Mia tips back so far she nearly falls. Leo grabs his notebook and tries to draw it
immediately. Sunny shouts the word "airplane" and they all repeat it, eyes on the sky.
Still no letter A is mentioned.

**Sample dialogue:**
> LEO: "Look look look! Up there — airplane!"
> SUNNY: "An airplane! Wow — airplane!"

---

### Beat 4 — 1:25 to 1:45 (20 seconds)
**Purpose:** Both objects side by side. Warm mistake-and-correction. Required research beat.

**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH (returned to Nana's porch with the apple; airplane still visible in sky)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Nana Blossom (max 4)

**Action:**
Sunny holds the apple in one hand and points up at the airplane with the other. She says she noticed
something — both of these are her favorites today. She asks the crew if they notice anything.
Leo squints thoughtfully, adjusts his glasses, and with great confidence declares: "They're both red!"
Sunny and Nana Blossom share a warm smile. Sunny says Leo's right that lots of wonderful things are red —
but there's something even more amazing. She gives Leo a proud nod. The correction is never about being
wrong; it's about finding something more amazing.

**Sample dialogue:**
> SUNNY: "Apple and airplane — I see something. Do you?"
> LEO: "They're both red! Both red!"
> SUNNY: "Leo — yes! Red IS amazing. And there's more!"

---

### Beat 5 — 1:45 to 2:00 (15 seconds)
**Purpose:** Letter A revealed. First mention of the letter in the episode. Nana Blossom delivers it.

**Location:** LOC_NANA_PORCH
**Characters present:** Sunny, Mia, Nana Blossom (max 4 — Leo can be present)

**Action:**
Nana Blossom crouches to the children's level — her signature listening pose. She picks up the apple
in one hand and gestures toward the sky (airplane) with the other. She says them slowly and warmly:
"Apple. Airplane." Then she looks at the children with her big warm eyes and says both start with the
same letter — A. The Letter A badge animates into the upper corner of the frame. Sunny's face goes
through pure wonder to pure joy. This is the cognitive click moment.

**Sample dialogue:**
> NANA BLOSSOM: "Apple. Airplane. Both start with A."
> SUNNY: "A! They both start with A!"

---

### Beat 6 — 2:00 to 2:15 (15 seconds)
**Purpose:** Call-and-response with the viewer. Required research beat. 3-second pause required.

**Location:** LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS (Sunny facing the camera directly)
**Characters present:** Sunny (direct address — crew visible but not featured)

**Action:**
Sunny turns fully to the camera and leans slightly forward. She tells the viewer she needs their help —
she wants to hear them say the letter. She asks clearly: "Can you say A?" She cups her hand to her ear
and waits. The full 3-second pause holds — the camera stays on Sunny's patient, warm, listening face.
Then she celebrates them big: hears them, names them, claps for them.

**Sample dialogue:**
> SUNNY: "Can you say it? Say A!"
> [3-SECOND PAUSE — child says "A" at home]
> SUNNY: "I HEARD you! You said A! Yes!"

---

### Beat 7 — 2:15 to 2:25 (10 seconds)
**Purpose:** Energy-build toward the song. Song is suggested by a crew member, not imposed.

**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET (sidewalk — full crew gathering)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Mia, Koda (can add Leo if desired; max 4)

**Action:**
Mimi runs into the sidewalk area spinning with her arms out like an airplane. She stops, claps her hands
three times, and points at Sunny. Koda, who has been watching from his doorstep, jogs over and says they
should sing about it. Energy rises — the suggestion comes from the crew, not just Sunny. Sunny's eyes light up.

**Sample dialogue:**
> MIMI: "Sing! Sing! Sing about A!"
> KODA: "We need the A song right now!"

---

### Beat 8 — 2:25 to 2:44 (19 seconds)
**Purpose:** Pre-song launch. Full crew gathers. Sunny counts in. Song begins.

**Location:** LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET (sidewalk — everyone gathered)
**Characters present:** Sunny, Leo, Mia, Koda (max 4 for the launch shot)

**Action:**
Everyone lines up shoulder to shoulder facing the camera. Sunny steps forward and holds out both hands
toward the viewer — an invitation, not a performance. She tells the viewer to sing with them. She draws
a breath, counts in with a grin, and the music begins. The screen flashes to white on the count-in.

**Sample dialogue:**
> SUNNY: "Sing with us — A is amazing!"
> SUNNY: "Ready? One, two, three — here we go!"
> [STAR BURST TRANSITION — music begins: A Is Amazing]

## SECTION 4 — WRAP-UP DIALOGUE

> Research Principle 4: The goodbye must feel earned — the child did something. Explicit pride
> must be stated. Take-home challenge extends learning beyond the episode.

**Scene: LOC_SUNNY_PORCH | Characters: Sunny (lead) + Leo (supporting)**
**Target runtime: 30 seconds | Maximum 8 words per line**

---

```
SUNNY
(seated on porch step, facing camera, warm and unhurried)
"You did it. I am SO proud of you."
```

```
SUNNY
(holds up one finger — deliberate pace, not rushing)
"We learned the letter A today."
```

```
SUNNY
(holds up one finger — crunch gesture)
"A is for apple!"
```

```
SUNNY
(holds up two fingers — wings-out dip)
"A is for airplane!"
```

```
LEO
(adjusting glasses, warm quiet smile)
"And A is for amazing — like us!"
```

```
SUNNY
(leaning slightly toward camera, voice soft, sincere)
"Exactly like us. Exactly like YOU."
```

```
SUNNY
(pause — then bright, forward energy, the challenge delivered as a gift)
"Here is your challenge — your A mission:"
"Find something at home that starts with A!"
```

```
SUNNY
(wide warm grin)
"Can you find it? I know you can."
"You are amazing. Bye bye!"
```
