# Shot Planning Template (per shot record)
shot_id (EP##-P#-S## or T##/A## for songs) | part | start/end (from cue sheet) | duration 3-5s | purpose (establish/emotion/interaction/support_lyric/participation/transition/outro) | location_id | characters (with element UUIDs) | action (one sentence, one event) | emotion | camera (angle+move, ONE move max) | motif (if any, per world rules) | lyric/dialogue line | kid_cue | asset_source (reuse id | needs_new) | transition (cut default).
Design rules: one purpose per shot; max 4 principal characters unless ensemble beat; direct-to-camera reserved for invitations, questions, praise, goodbyes; scale rules always (Mimi smallest, Nana tallest); no Bella+Commander; silhouette-readable staging; palette consistent; no clutter (max 3 focal props).
