# Asset Reuse Policy
ALWAYS reuse: THEME-INTRO-MASTER.mp4 (every episode); locked character elements; location establishing stills as start_images for same-location shots; finished song sections in compilations; music beds across episodes (practice/hunt beds are show-level assets after first creation).
NEVER reuse: any video clip twice within an episode; another episode's clips (except compilations of finished masters); regenerating a locked design without creator approval.
Before generating anything: check trackers + Higgsfield history + Drive (VERIFICATION_PROTOCOL) for an existing usable asset.
