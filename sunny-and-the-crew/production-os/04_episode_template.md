# Episode Template (locked 6-part structure)
P1 THEME (0:45 fixed): THEME-INTRO-MASTER.mp4 prepended in CI. Never regenerated.
P2 STORY SETUP (~1:30 std / up to 2:30 ext): object-first discovery beats. REQUIRED: (a) both/all lesson objects experienced BEFORE the concept is named, (b) WARM MISTAKE beat (guess wrong -> discover better, never shame), (c) concept reveal by a mentor or discovery moment with badge/chalk motif, (d) CALL-AND-RESPONSE with explicit [3-SECOND PAUSE] marker + celebration of the viewer's answer.
P3 SONG LEAD-IN (~0:30): song requested BY the crew (never imposed), movement cues primed, Sunny count-in, star-burst transition.
P4 EPISODE SONG (actual Suno length): visuals beat-mapped to REAL waveform (song_sections.json per episode). Movement cue every chorus. EXTENDED format adds AFTER the song, in order: Echo Practice (phonics/word echo w/ pauses), Word/Concept Hunt mini-game (3 finds, each with question + 3s pause), Movement Reprise (song replays with all-new instructional dance-along clips — audio reuse OK, clip reuse NEVER).
P5 WRAP-UP (0:30-1:10): recap with gestures, explicit viewer praise ("I heard you!"), Heart Pop allowed here, TAKE-HOME CHALLENGE delivered as a gift, goodbye wave.
P6 CLOSING STING (0:08): logo center, ukulele hit, Sunny wave, warm fade.
Clip rules: 3-5s per clip (theme cuts + single-shot intros exempt when bible-locked); no clip repeats; simple cuts; star-burst only into songs; warm fade only at end.
