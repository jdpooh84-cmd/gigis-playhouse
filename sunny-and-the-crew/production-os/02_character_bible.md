IDs + locked element UUIDs live in characters.json. This file adds performance rules.

## Scale & Pairing Rules (HARD)
- MIMI: always the smallest character on screen.
- NANA BLOSSOM: always the tallest character on screen.
- BELLA and COMMANDER: never in the same shot. Bella is NOT Commander.
- KODA IS A BOY (Sunny's older brother). Commander is the dog. Any doc calling Koda a dog is wrong.
- RENA: multicolor paint smudge on LEFT cheek, always present.
- MAYOR MARY: short full auburn red curly hair — restate in every prompt she appears in.

## Performance Bible
SUNNY (lead) — curious, brave, warm. Models asking out loud. Catchphrase: "Let's ask! Let's find out! Come on, Crew!" Poses: arms-wide invitation, lean-in secret share, ear-cup listening, thumbs-up praise, two-hand goodbye wave. Emotional triggers: viewer answering (joy burst), friend discovery (sun-burst moment). Never sarcastic, never impatient.
KODA — The Builder. Confident, jumps in unasked. Signature: fist pump; cool heel-click; steady big-brother nod. Speaks in short can-do lines ("On it!" "We can build that!").
MIMI — The Reactor. Attachment character for 2-3s. NEARLY WORDLESS: max 6 words per line, words repeated in threes ("Sing! Sing! Sing!"). Everything else is reaction: gasp, sparkle-eyes, tiny knowing smile, jump-for-joy, finger-to-chin. Camera gives her reaction cutaways at least once per episode. Wears her floral sneakers (old barefoot rule retired by creator 2026-07-10).
LEO RIVERA — co-lead, Big Dreamer. Energetic, heart-forward, rockets + notebook always. Makes the warm mistakes most often — and loves discovering better.
MIA CHEN — Thoughtful Strategist. Notices the detail that unlocks the lesson. Speaks second, not first. Quiet confidence; big payoff smiles.
PIPA — The Dramatic One. BEST EVER or WORST EVER. Arms flung wide or full pout; recovers instantly.
BRAM — The Thinker. Chin-tap concentration, calm delivery, quiet-but-full celebrations. Pipa's twin; together they model opposite temperaments as best friends.
NANA BLOSSOM — mentor. NEVER gives the answer first; asks the question that lets kids find it. Crouches to child eye level for reveals. Belly laugh, hands on hips.
MAYOR MARY — warm authority (based on creator's grandmother). Clear, kind, gets things done.
AVA (co-op teacher), RICO (sporty, generous with effort), RENA (always making something), RANDY (corner store, knows everyone), ANNE (music teacher).
BELLA — Wise Wanderer cat. Appears when needed; one slow blink = approval. Mystery thread; never explained.
COMMANDER — Koda's spotted dog. Always happy, underfoot, accidentally helpful. Comic relief; never in danger, never scolded harshly.

## Dialogue Voice Rules
Max 8 words per line for all kids. Mimi max 6 with triple-repeat cadence. Nana speaks slowly, warmly, fewer words than you think. No character ever mocks another.
