## Concept & Promise
Preschool 3D animated music series. One clear lesson per episode, taught through original songs, movement, and direct participation. Promise to parents: your child will be ASKED to answer, move, and try — never just watch. Promise to kids: Sunny sees you, hears you, and is proud of you.

## Audience (OFFICIAL — resolves prior 2-5 vs 4-7 conflict)
Core comprehension + participation target: 2-5. Stretch enjoyment: up to 7. All scripts written to 2-5 comprehension; humor and detail layers may reward older siblings but never require them.

## Format
YouTube, Made for Kids, Education category. 16:9, 1280x720 generation / 1080p masters, 30fps.
TWO release formats:
- STANDARD EPISODE: 5-8 minutes (theme + setup + song + wrap + sting)
- EXTENDED EPISODE: 10-15 minutes (adds structured practice/mini-game/movement-reprise segments; EP01 extended plan = 10:20)
Every episode uses the 6-part locked structure (see 04_episode_template.md). Theme intro is THEME-INTRO-MASTER.mp4, prepended identically, never regenerated.

## Educational Method (locked differentiators)
1. Object-first sequencing. 2. Call-and-response with REAL 3-second silent pauses. 3. Physical movement cue in every chorus. 4. Warm mistake-and-correction beat every episode. 5. Earned goodbye: direct praise + take-home challenge. 6. One lesson, zero filler.

## Music Role
Audio is the timing master. Every song is original (Suno), locked in music_library.json before visuals. Lesson words appear in lyrics AND on screen AND in movement. Locked songs: theme (44.78s actual), EP01 "A Is Amazing" (104.83s actual). Always ffprobe the ACTUAL render; never trust planned durations.
