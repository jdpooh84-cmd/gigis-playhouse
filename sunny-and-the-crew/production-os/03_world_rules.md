# World Rules — Marigold Grove (v1.0)
## Map (3-block radius; a preschooler can hold it in their head)
Main street: Sunny's house (porch = direct-address space; backyard = core play space: sunflower garden, red picnic table, white fence). Same street: Nana Blossom's porch + garden; Randy's corner store. One block over: homeschool co-op classroom + playground. Three-minute walk: the park. Community center on the square (Mayor Mary).
Location IDs: LOC_SUNNY_PORCH, LOC_BACKYARD, LOC_NEIGHBORHOOD_STREET, LOC_NANA_PORCH, LOC_GARDEN, LOC_CLASSROOM, LOC_COOP_PLAYGROUND, LOC_PARK, LOC_COMMUNITY_CENTER, LOC_CORNER_STORE, LOC_DIRECT_ADDRESS (warm yellow gradient).
## Travel (HARD)
Foot or bicycle only. NO CARS EVER on screen. No busy roads, no traffic.
## Light & Color (HARD)
Default warm afternoon sun, soft directional from camera-right; zero dark areas in any child-visible frame. Locked 8-color palette (Sunny Yellow #FFCC00, Sky Blue #7EC8E3, Grass Green #6ABF69, Warm Coral #FF7F5C, Cream White #FFF8ED, Mia Teal #2EC4B6, Golden Brown #C47A35, Leo Red #CC2200). Prohibited: harsh shadows, looming/low-angle threat compositions, desaturated scenes, photoreal textures, adult-coded references, unnamed detailed background people (stylized rounded forms only).
## Motif Usage Rules
Sun-burst: only behind Sunny, only on aha moments. Letter badge: first letter reveal only. Chalk words: every new concept noun, wobbly child-drawn font, coral. Wonder dots -> lightbulb: thinking beats. Note trails: songs only, drift matches BPM. Heart Pop: MAX once per episode. Bounce Underscore: every chorus downbeat, 105%->100%, felt not seen. Sparkle trail (cyan): crew assembly moments.
## Time
Perpetual golden afternoon; golden-hour warmth allowed for bridge/outro emotional beats. No night scenes in Season 1.
