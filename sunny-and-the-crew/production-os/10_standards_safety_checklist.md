# Standards & Safety Checklist (S&P pass)
- Age-appropriate 2-5 at all times; nothing requiring reading to follow the story.
- No imitable unsafe behavior: no car interaction (no cars at all), no dangerous climbing/jumping from height, nothing put in mouths except clearly-food, no running into streets.
- Emotional safety: no character shamed, mocked, excluded, or left sad at a cut; all conflict resolved warmly on screen; no peril, chase-fear, or menace.
- Overstimulation guard: shots >=3s (theme cuts exempt), max one camera move per shot, no strobe/flash beyond the single white star-burst (<0.5s), bounce subtle.
- Platform trust: Made for Kids on; no loop-bait, no "don't skip!", no manipulative retention devices; thumbnails/titles truthful.
- IP: zero competitor references (names, lyrics, catchphrases, logos, soundalikes).
- Data/COPPA posture: no CTAs to comment/subscribe directed at kids; parent-directed lines only in description text.
