# Compilation Strategy
After 3+ episodes: "Sing-Along Collection" videos = theme intro + N song sections back-to-back (each with its count-in lead clip), 15-30 min, titled honestly ("3 Songs to Learn Letters A-B-C"). After 5 episodes: domain compilations (All Letter Songs / All Counting Songs). Rules: reuse finished masters only (no regeneration), keep each song's movement cues intact, insert 2s breathing gap card (logo on cream) between songs, new metadata per 08 template. Shorts: 9:16 crops of single choruses with the movement cue, one per episode, pointing to full episode.
